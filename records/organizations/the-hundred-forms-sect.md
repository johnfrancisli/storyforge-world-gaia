The wandering chi-cultivation sect that trains martial monks and chi-healers across the temples of Yunmeng's Hundred Gods quarter, counting the wandering monk Jian Yi, the chi-cultivation nun Ye Qingrong, and the disguised master Niu Caihong among its hidden ranks. The sect teaches non-interference in the three-states war, yet chi-cultivators are being secretly conscripted as living weapons, and the child prodigy Lin Jiaojiao is the prize all three states now hunt. The sect wants to keep chi-cultivation knowledge out of the warlords' armies and protect the ungifted prodigy from being weaponized.
