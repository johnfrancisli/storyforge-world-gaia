The great spice merchant houses that control the saffron, myrrh, and rare-spice trade from the Saffron Palace in [[location:zaffar|Zaffar]], where Layla the Spice Queen holds council alongside the political agitator [[character:yasmin-al-barrani|Yasmin al-Barrani]], the rare-spice trader [[character:fatima-bint-idris|Fatima bint Idris]], and the binding-family informant [[character:walid-al-tujjar|Walid al-Tujjar]]. The houses vie to fix prices and corner the caravan routes, yet Yasmin agitates openly against the binding families' monopoly on djinn-power while respectable houses like Layla's beg her to keep trade and politics apart. The houses want to break the binding families' grip on city governance and put spice-coin on the ruling council.
