The caravan companies that run the desert trade routes between the oasis-cities and beyond, mustered at the Caravanserai of a Thousand Saddles under the desert guide Rashid the Sand-walker, the guard captain Karim al-Qafila, and the porter Omar al-Hamil. The companies pride themselves on safe passage, but banditry on the Broken Road is rising and Karim is, unknowingly, a djinn agent, so some masters pay the djinn for protection while others refuse to truck with bound spirits. The companies want to secure the desert routes against bandits and djinn meddling and keep the caravans moving.
