Valdris's border military jurisdiction commanding the forward keeps along the Verdmarch. Held by the High Constable, an office that rotates between noble houses every five years. The Constabulary is underfunded, undermanned, and deeply cynical -- three generations of trench warfare without a single meter of gained ground will do that. The sigil is a crowned iron tower split by a downward broadsword. The motto is 'Not One Span Yielded,' and the soldiers add their own line under their breath: 'and not one span taken either.'
