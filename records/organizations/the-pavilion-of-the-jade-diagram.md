The circle of military strategists and scholar-advisors who convene at the [[location:san-chudu-jade-dragon-pavilion|Jade Dragon Pavilion]] in [[location:san-chudu|Chudu]], including the Wei master strategist [[character:cao-mengshi|Cao Mengshi]], the Jin junior advisor [[character:mei-lin|Mei Lin]], and the Wei defector-in-waiting [[character:he-jingtian|He Jingtian]]. The circle's oath is to the realm rather than any lord, yet every strategist secretly serves a state, and defectors like [[character:he-jingtian|He Jingtian]] are hunted by the patrons they betrayed. The circle wants to preserve itself as a neutral brain-trust so that strategy, not mere force, decides the war.
