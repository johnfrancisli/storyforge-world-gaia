The circle of military strategists and scholar-advisors who convene at the Jade Dragon Pavilion in Chudu, including the Wei master strategist Cao Mengshi, the Jin junior advisor Mei Lin, and the Wei defector-in-waiting He Jingtian. The circle's oath is to the realm rather than any lord, yet every strategist secretly serves a state, and defectors like He Jingtian are hunted by the patrons they betrayed. The circle wants to preserve itself as a neutral brain-trust so that strategy, not mere force, decides the war.
