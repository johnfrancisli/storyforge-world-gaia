The council of jarls that meets at the Thing-Mound of Eyrby to settle feuds, elect the High Jarl, and vote on the summer raids, where Jarl Sigrid Hallvarsdottir of Skaldvik holds the High Seat against the young Jarl Erik Stone-eye of the southern fjords, with Gerd Hallvarsdottir speaking for the farming thralls. Sigrid preaches caution while Erik gathers raiders hungry for southern plunder, and the council splits cleanly down that fault line. The Thing wants to keep the peace among the jarls and prevent a civil war over the High Seat.
