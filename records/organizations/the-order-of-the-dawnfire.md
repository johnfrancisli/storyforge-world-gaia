The kingdom's dominant religious order, centered on the Cathedral of the Dawnfire in [[location:crownhaven|Crownhaven]], where priest [[character:evangeline-greymantle|Evangeline Greymantle]] and the monk-scribe [[character:galen-frosthollow|Galen Frosthollow]] serve its chapters. The church blesses the Dragon Order's knights as divinely chosen, yet a reformist faction of priests asks openly whether dragon-bonding is holy or heretical, splitting every cathedral chapter down the middle. The order wants Crown recognition as the sole legitimate faith and tithes from every holding in the realm.
