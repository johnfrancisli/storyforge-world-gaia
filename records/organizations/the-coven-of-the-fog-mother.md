The covens of völur seeresses who read the runes and cast the futures from the Bone-Circle at [[location:hra-hvalhofn|Hvalhöfn]], where the elder Volva Helga and the rune-reader [[character:yrsa-fogborn|Yrsa Fogborn]] speak for the sight. The völur see a coming doom — a long winter and a war the jarls will not survive — but the jarls distrust prophecy and the young raiders mock the old women, splitting the coven between those who warn openly and those who retreat into silence. The coven wants to make the jarls heed the warning visions and prepare the folk for the long winter before it breaks.
