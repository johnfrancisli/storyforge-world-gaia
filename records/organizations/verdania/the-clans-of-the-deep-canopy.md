The coalition of deep-forest hunter clans — uniting jaguar-kin, feline cat-folk pathfinders, and their human allies — who provide meat, pelts, and forest-patrol for the canopy tribes, with [[character:kaa|Kaa]], [[character:miri|Miri]], the cat-folk tracker [[character:roca|Roça]], the path-reader [[character:boca|Boca]], and the night-patrol guard [[character:urutu|Urutu]] among their number. The older hunters honor the jaguar-spirit pact and take only what the forest offers, while younger hunters over-hunt to feed the canopy market's hunger for pelts and rare parts. The clans want to enforce the old hunting pacts and keep outsider traders out of the deep forest before the spirits turn vengeful.
