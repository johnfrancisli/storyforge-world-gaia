# The Blood-Iron Company

The Blood-Iron Company is the most elite, expensive, and ruthlessly effective mercenary company operating across Gaia. They are heavily active along the contested borders, frequently seen guarding merchant caravans through the [[location:border-silk-pass|Silk Pass]] or taking highly-paid defensive contracts along the edges of the Verdmarch.

## Composition and Code
The Company is composed entirely of exiles, disgraced nobles, and veterans who have survived the worst conflicts in Gaia. There is no discrimination of origin; a [[location:sangguo|Sangguo]] martial artist might fight in a shield wall next to a disgraced [[location:valdris|Valdris]] knight or a blood-owed Hrafnland raider.

Their defining characteristic is their absolute, unbreakable adherence to the contract. Once a client's gold is paid and the contract is signed, the Blood-Iron Company will not break it—not for a better offer, not for a plea of mercy, and not to save their own lives. A king could double their fee to turn on their employer, and the Company would slaughter the king's envoy for the insult.

## Combat Doctrine
Because they recruit from all nations, their combat doctrine is a terrifying synthesis of global warfare. They combine Valdrian shield-wall tactics with Sangguo chi-cultivated shock troops and Hrafnland berserker skirmishers. They are heavily armored, highly disciplined, and completely devoid of political allegiance.

Their banner is a plain iron shield on a field of dark red. When that banner appears on a battlefield, it usually means someone with very deep pockets has decided the battle is over before it begins.
