The merchant guild (akindo) that controls coastal trade through [[location:tsu-kawazaki-great-harbor|Kawazaki Great Harbor]], where respectable merchants like Machi chase shogunate trade charters while smugglers like Goro run Takeda-sympathetic contraband through the same docks. The guild looks the other way on smuggling for its cut, but the split between charter-merchants and smugglers widens as the shogunate tightens its grip on the harbor. The league wants to monopolize the sea-lane trade and keep the harbor free of direct shogunate interference.
