Mercenary hosts that hire their shields to southern wars during the winter thaw. The Boneharrow Companies are Hrafnland's most reliable export -- trained, hardened fighters who take contracts in Valdris, Sangguo, and occasionally Al-Khayzar. They are not pirates; they are professionals. They fight for the contract, not the cause, and they honor the terms precisely. A company that breaks a contract is blacklisted across Hrafnland and cannot hire again.
