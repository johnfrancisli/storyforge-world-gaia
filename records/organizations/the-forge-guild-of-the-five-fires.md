The swordsmiths and blacksmiths of the Kaeden Forge Quarter who forge the blades of the samurai class, with blacksmith Kazue among their working smiths. The guild is bound by charter to supply the Ashikara shogunate first, but Takeda samurai demand blades too, and some smiths quietly forge for both sides to survive the clans' rivalry. The guild wants to protect its secret tempering techniques and avoid being conscripted into open war between the clans.
