The mercantile cartels controlling [[location:verdania|Verdania]]'s coastal ports, wine cellars, and trade houses. They produce verdemiel -- the first-pressed floral wine that is [[location:verdania|Verdania]]'s most valuable export. The Vintner Guilds are urbane, perceptive, and richer than most noble houses. They balance decadent trade with ancestral herbal secrets, and they have no love for the Briar Wardens, whose border war disrupts the northern trade routes.
