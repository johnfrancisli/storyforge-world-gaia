The merchant cartel of the Five Houses that controls the Silk Road trade through Yunmeng, where information-broker Merchant Zhou Ba plays all three warring states against each other and tea-merchant Su Wanqing feeds the Academy intelligence in parallel. The cartel profits only so long as Jin, Chu, and Wei remain divided enough to outbid each other for silk and spice, and a single misread shipment could bring a warlord's army down on the bazaar. The cartel wants to keep the silk and spice roads open and the three states balanced in perpetual rivalry.
