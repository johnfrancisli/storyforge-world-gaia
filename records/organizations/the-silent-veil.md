# The Silent Veil

The Silent Veil is an ancient order of *kage-bito* (shadow-agents) based in [[location:tsukuyomi|Tsukuyomi]], though their reach extends across the Moon Strait to [[location:verdania|Verdania]] and across the Long Passage to [[location:sangguo|Sangguo]]. They are not mere assassins; they are an organization that views death as a necessary spiritual balancing of the kami-order.

## Philosophy and Doctrine
To the Silent Veil, a life taken is not a murder but a transaction—a debt collected on behalf of the spirits, or a rebalancing of *shinryoku* (spirit-resonance). The order demands total detachment from its initiates. A true agent of the Veil has no family, no clan, and eventually, no face.

They use a highly specialized, secretive branch of shrine magic combined with forbidden yokai pacts to literally strip away their own identities. In doing so, they become perfect vessels for assassination, able to wear the faces and voices of others, slipping through castle-cities and shrine-towns unnoticed.

## Operations
The Veil does not work for coin alone. A clan seeking their services must offer something of profound spiritual weight: an ancestral relic, a sacred oath (often a *tsukiyo-chikai*, an oath made under the open moon), or the promise of a future life. If the Veil accepts the contract, the target is already considered dead; it is only a matter of time.

They are feared throughout the [[org:ashikara-shogunate|Ashikara Shogunate]]. Even the most powerful warlords sleep with wards on their doors, not against rival armies, but against a servant who might suddenly pour tea with a stranger's eyes.
