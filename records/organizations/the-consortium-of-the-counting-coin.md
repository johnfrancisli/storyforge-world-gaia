The merchant guild spanning Rivenspire's counting houses and Crossford's trade roads, controlling river traffic and the crossroad tolls that fund the realm's commerce. Mead-brewer and merchant Becka Honeymead is one of its many trade-house members. River-merchants in Rivenspire want the tolls kept low while guildmasters in Crossford raise them, and the Greyfen thieves' guild skims from nearly every shipment that passes through. The consortium wants a monopoly on Valdris river-trade and the crossroad tolls that come with it.
