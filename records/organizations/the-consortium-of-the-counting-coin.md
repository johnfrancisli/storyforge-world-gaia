The merchant guild spanning [[location:val-rivenspire|Rivenspire]]'s counting houses and [[location:val-crossford|Crossford]]'s trade roads, controlling river traffic and the crossroad tolls that fund the realm's commerce. Mead-brewer and merchant [[character:becka-honeymead|Becka Honeymead]] is one of its many trade-house members. River-merchants in [[location:val-rivenspire|Rivenspire]] want the tolls kept low while guildmasters in [[location:val-crossford|Crossford]] raise them, and the Greyfen thieves' guild skims from nearly every shipment that passes through. The consortium wants a monopoly on Valdris river-trade and the crossroad tolls that come with it.
