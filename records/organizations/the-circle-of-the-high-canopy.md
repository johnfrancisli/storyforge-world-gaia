The secluded council of sylvan forest elves hidden among the highest kapok and ironwood branches of [[location:verdania|Verdania]]. Guided by elder botanist [[character:lyra-moonleaf|Lyra Moonleaf]], with spirit-weaver [[character:sylva-orchid|Sylva Orchid]], sacred root tender [[character:faena-of-the-canopy|Faena of the Canopy]], master fletcher [[character:aerion-greenbough|Aerion Greenbough]], branch runner [[character:kalan-riverfern|Kalan Riverfern]], and mist stalker [[character:thalan-mistrunner|Thalan Mistrunner]], the Circle preserves ancient botanical remedies and protects the deep canopy. They maintain a sacred covenant of root and river with the river shaman [[character:nara|Nara]], ensuring the rainforest's lifeblood remains unbroken.
