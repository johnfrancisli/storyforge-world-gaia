An order of master wayfinders who have gone beyond navigation into the deeper applications of song-magic. The Deep Water Singers can call whales, read currents across a thousand miles of open ocean, and sing storms calm -- or call them. They are few, old, and increasingly worried. The songs are not working the way they used to. The currents are shifting. The whales are leaving. Something is changing in the deep water, and the singers do not know what.
