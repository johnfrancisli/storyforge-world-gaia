The council of island chiefs that meets to settle disputes between atolls, allocate fishing grounds, and decide the fate of the drowned-island refugees, led by Chief Lani of [[location:lani-atoll|Lani Atoll]] alongside the refugee former chief Ariki Lost-Chief. The council must decide whether to grant the refugees permanent land or send them onward, and Chief Lani's generosity strains her own people's patience while displaced chiefs like Ariki demand a voice the council is reluctant to give. The council wants to keep the peace among the atolls and find a lasting home for the refugees without breaking the host islands.
