The circle of hedge-mages, healers, and seers rooted in the elven Groveveil Sanctum of Elfshade, counting librarian Elenna Moonwhisper, fortune-teller Roslyn Vale, healer Isolde Ravenwood, and the hermit elder Rowan Silverbranch among its number. Human mages chafe at the elven elders' caution about teaching higher magic, and the Crown fears unregulated sorcery enough that the Dragon Order keeps the circle under watch. The circle wants a royal charter to practice magic freely, without Dragon Order oversight.
