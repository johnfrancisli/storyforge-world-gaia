Mercenary hosts that hire their shields to southern wars during the winter thaw. The Boneharrow Companies are [[location:hrafnland|Hrafnland]]'s most reliable export -- trained, hardened fighters who take contracts in Valdris, [[location:sangguo|Sangguo]], and occasionally [[location:al-khayzar|Al-Khayzar]]. They are not pirates; they are professionals. They fight for the contract, not the cause, and they honor the terms precisely. A company that breaks a contract is blacklisted across [[location:hrafnland|Hrafnland]] and cannot hire again.
