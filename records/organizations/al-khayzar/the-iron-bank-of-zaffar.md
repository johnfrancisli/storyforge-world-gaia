# The Iron Bank of Zaffar

Headquartered in the free city of [[location:zaffar|Zaffar]] in the southern deserts of [[location:al-khayzar|Al-Khayzar]], the Iron Bank is arguably the most powerful financial institution in Gaia. They do not field armies, yet they hold the debts of warlords in [[location:sangguo|Sangguo]], kings in [[location:valdris|Valdris]], and jarls in [[location:hrafnland|Hrafnland]].

## The Power of the Vault
The Bank's true power lies in its monopoly on high-level, inter-national debt, enforced through a terrifying application of djinn-binding. While traditional [[org:binding-families|Binding Families]] use djinn for protection, trade, or political leverage, the Iron Bank binds djinn specifically as auditors and debt collectors.

When a nation or powerful guild takes a loan from the Iron Bank, the contract is signed in blood and witnessed by an ancient fire-spirit. These contracts are ironclad. If a debt is defaulted upon, the Bank does not send soldiers; it sends the bound djinn. The collection is immediate, supernatural, and without mercy, often taking the form of spontaneous fires in the debtor's vaults or the sudden, inexplicable death of their firstborn.

## Neutrality and Ruthlessness
The Iron Bank maintains strict political neutrality. They will fund both sides of a war across the Verdmarch if the terms are favorable. To the Bank, morality is a liability; mathematics and the absolute sanctity of the contract are the only truths.

Their sprawling, heavily fortified headquarters in Zaffar is said to hold vaults guarded not by locks, but by centuries-old pacts that would incinerate anyone attempting to steal a single coin without authorization.
