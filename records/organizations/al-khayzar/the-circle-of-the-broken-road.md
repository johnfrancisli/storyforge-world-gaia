The circle of scholars, astrologers, and mathematicians who preserve learning at the Archive of the Broken Road in Khalesh, where the mathematician [[character:maha-al-hisab|Maha al-Hisab]], the true-name astrologer [[character:tara-al-nujum|Tara al-Nujum]], the information-broker astrologer [[character:imran-al-nujum|Imran al-Nujum]], and the elder [[character:yusuf-al-hakim|Yusuf al-Hakim]] — living archive of the Pact — keep the old knowledge. The scholars seek truth, but the binding families and spice houses want them to justify political claims, and those who quietly study the djinn-Pact's loopholes risk the binding families' wrath. The circle wants free inquiry under a neutral patron and access to the sealed Pact archives.
