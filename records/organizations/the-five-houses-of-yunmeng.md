The merchant cartel of the Five Houses that controls the Silk Road trade through [[location:san-silkroad-city|Yunmeng]], where information-broker Merchant [[character:zhou-ba|Zhou Ba]] plays all three warring states against each other and tea-merchant [[character:su-wanqing|Su Wanqing]] feeds the Academy intelligence in parallel. The cartel profits only so long as Jin, Chu, and Wei remain divided enough to outbid each other for silk and spice, and a single misread shipment could bring a warlord's army down on the bazaar. The cartel wants to keep the silk and spice roads open and the three states balanced in perpetual rivalry.
