The guild of pearl divers who descend the deep reefs for the trade-pearls that are the Archipelago's chief wealth, counting Hina Deepwater, the deep-lunged Vili Deep-Lung, the apprentice Tama Pearl-Blood, and the pearl trader Lio Deep-Pearl who sells their harvest abroad. The divers risk the bends and shark-waters for pearls that traders like Lio sell for a profit the divers rarely share, and the deep reefs yield fewer pearls each season. The guild wants a fair share of the pearl trade and protected diving grounds before the deep reefs are exhausted.
