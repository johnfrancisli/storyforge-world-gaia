The kingdom's dominant religious order, centered on the Cathedral of the Dawnfire in Crownhaven, where priest Evangeline Greymantle and the monk-scribe Galen Frosthollow serve its chapters. The church blesses the Dragon Order's knights as divinely chosen, yet a reformist faction of priests asks openly whether dragon-bonding is holy or heretical, splitting every cathedral chapter down the middle. The order wants Crown recognition as the sole legitimate faith and tithes from every holding in the realm.
