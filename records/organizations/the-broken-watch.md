# The Broken Watch

The Broken Watch was once a proud extension of the [[location:val-northwatch|Northwatch]] garrison in [[location:valdris|Valdris]], a noble order of knights and men-at-arms sworn by the [[org:val-dawnfire-church|Order of the Dawnfire]] to guard the northern shores against raiders from [[location:hrafnland|Hrafnland]].

## The Fall from Grace
Over the centuries, as the Valdrian kingdom focused its resources on internal disputes and the Verdmarch border in the south, the northern garrisons were slowly starved of funding and respect. The noble knights stopped coming, and the watch-posts along the Basalt Skerries were instead manned by criminals given a choice between the executioner's block and the freezing rain of the northern shore.

Today, The Broken Watch is a survivalist fraternity. The oaths they swear are no longer to the Dawnfire or the King, but to each other, forged in the bitter cold and the shared misery of the skerries.

## Operations and Corruption
While they nominally still watch the channels for Hrafnland longships, they are just as likely to take a bribe from a raider as they are to sound the alarm. They control the flow of black-market goods across the frozen straits, trading Valdrian iron for Hrafnland furs and illicit runic totems.

Their fortresses—squat, wind-blasted stone towers perched on jagged basalt—are places of grim, violent justice. A man who steals from his brother in the Watch is often thrown into the freezing sea; a man who holds the line in a skirmish earns a place by the fire. It is an ugly, brutal existence, far removed from the chivalric ideals that built their towers.
