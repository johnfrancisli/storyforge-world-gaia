The guardian order that protects the ruined [[location:ver-templeruins|Temple of the Coiled Serpent]] and the spirit-knowledge sealed within it, led from the pyramid's Serpent's Crown by the warning-keeper Zel of the Temple and the chief's guard [[character:sangue|Sangue]]. The guardians are sworn to keep outsiders from the summit, but the river-traders and the academic explorer Dr. Voss press to study the ruins, and a faction believes the serpent spirit must be re-awakened to defend the forest while the rest insist it stay sealed. The order wants to keep the serpent temple sealed and stop outsiders from plundering its spirit-marks.
