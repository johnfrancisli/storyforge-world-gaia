The guild of pearl divers who descend the deep reefs for the trade-pearls that are the Archipelago's chief wealth, counting agile cat-folk divers like [[character:hina|Hina]], the deep-lunged [[character:vili|Vili]], the apprentice [[character:tama|Tama]], and the pearl trader [[character:lio|Lio]] who sells their harvest abroad. The divers risk the bends and shark-waters for pearls that traders like Lio sell for a profit the divers rarely share, and the deep reefs yield fewer pearls each season. The guild wants a fair share of the pearl trade and protected diving grounds before the deep reefs are exhausted.
