# The Chain-Markets of Khalesh

Situated in the ancient, subterranean cistern-city of Khalesh in [[location:al-khayzar|Al-Khayzar]], the Chain-Markets are a massive, legally sanctioned institution that governs the buying and selling of human labor, up to and including permanent slavery.

## The Economy of Flesh
In a land where survival is harsh and magical power is literally bought by trading years of one's life to a djinn, the concept of selling one's physical body or labor is deeply normalized. The Chain-Markets do not operate in the shadows; they are an open, regulated, and heavily taxed organization. A person might sell themselves into ten years of servitude to pay a family debt, or a warlord might sell prisoners of war to fund their next campaign.

The Brokers who run the Markets are considered respectable, if ruthless, businessmen. They measure a person's worth by their skills, their health, and their magical affinity. A scholar from [[location:sangguo|Sangguo]] might fetch a high price as a tutor for a wealthy Al-Khayzari merchant, while a strong laborer is sold to work the deep water channels under the supervision of a *qanat-dar*.

## Regulation and Cruelty
The institution prides itself on "civilized" regulation. Contracts are drawn up detailing the length of servitude, acceptable punishments, and the conditions of release (if any). However, this bureaucratic veneer barely masks the underlying cruelty. Slaves are branded with magical markers to prevent escape, and families are routinely separated if the market demands it.

While the [[org:red-sands-syndicate|Red Sands Syndicate]] operates illegally and with unchecked brutality, the Chain-Markets of Khalesh are often more terrifying precisely because their brand of human suffering is completely legal, highly organized, and socially accepted.
