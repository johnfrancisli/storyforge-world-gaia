The guild of rune-carvers who cut the binding-runes on ships, weapons, and standing stones from their bothies at [[location:hra-runarholm|Rúnarhólm]], with [[character:haldur-blackrune|Haldur Blackrune]] and the bone-carver [[character:hjordis-bonebreeze|Hjordis Bonebreeze]] among its masters. The masters guard the true meanings of the First Runes, but younger carvers sell decorative runes to traders along the Amber-Iron Road, diluting the script's power for coin. The guild wants to keep the secret of the First Runes and stop the cheap-rune trade before it unravels the wards that hold the frost-giants at peace.
