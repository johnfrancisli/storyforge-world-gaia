A covert fellowship of non-human artisans—principally master dwarven jade-drill engineers like [[character:tie-luo|Tie Luo]] and master stonemasons like [[character:shi-bao|Shi Bao]]—forced into underground operation by Sangguo's quiet human-only state decrees. Operating through hidden mountain forges and sympathetic human guild fronts, they secretly service heavy hydraulic canal locks, mine elevators, and arched stone bridges that imperial human guilds lack the skill to maintain, relying on trusted allies such as the canal lock-keeper [[character:yang-fuhai|Yang Fuhai]].
