The administrative backbone of Sangguo -- the bureaucracy that manages tax registries, civil examinations, and espionage dossiers across all three states. The Jade Brush Ministry is technically neutral in the Jin-Chu-Wei rivalry, serving whichever state holds the current mandate. In practice, the ministry's loyalties are divided along regional lines, and clerks in Jin'an, Chudu, and Weidu serve different masters while wearing the same insignia. A Jade Brush clerk knows where the grain is, where the soldiers are, and where the money went. That knowledge is power.
