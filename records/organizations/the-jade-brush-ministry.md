The administrative backbone of [[location:sangguo|Sangguo]] -- the bureaucracy that manages tax registries, civil examinations, and espionage dossiers across all three states. The Jade Brush Ministry is technically neutral in the Jin-Chu-Wei rivalry, serving whichever state holds the current mandate. In practice, the ministry's loyalties are divided along regional lines, and clerks in [[location:jin-an|Jin'an]], [[location:san-chudu|Chudu]], and [[location:san-weidu|Weidu]] serve different masters while wearing the same insignia. A Jade Brush clerk knows where the grain is, where the soldiers are, and where the money went. That knowledge is power.
