The central ruling cabal of Tsukuyomi -- lunar priests and courtly nobility operating from the Shogunal Palace in [[location:tsuki-no-miyako|Tsuki-no-Miyako]]. The Pale Lotus Court controls imperial ceremony, the shrine network's funding, and the appointment of regional daimyo. They are refined, indirect, and lethal. A word dropped at tea can end a career. A silence held at the right moment can end a life. The Court's power is ceremonial on the surface and absolute beneath it.
