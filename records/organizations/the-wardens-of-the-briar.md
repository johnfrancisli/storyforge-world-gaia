[[location:verdania|Verdania]]'s border defense -- a guerrilla network of woodland sentries who contest Valdrian expansion along the northern border. They use living thorn barricades, deadfalls, and pit traps instead of stonework. The Briar Wardens are not soldiers; they are hunters, foresters, and shamans who know the canopy the way a sailor knows the sea. They do not fight in the open. They redirect, ambush, and let the jungle do the killing. The Valdrian trenches face an enemy they can rarely see.
