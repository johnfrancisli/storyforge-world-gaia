The circle of scholars, astrologers, and mathematicians who preserve learning at the Archive of the Broken Road in Khalesh, where the mathematician Maha al-Hisab, the true-name astrologer Tara al-Nujum, the information-broker astrologer Imran al-Nujum, and the elder Yusuf al-Hakim — living archive of the Pact — keep the old knowledge. The scholars seek truth, but the binding families and spice houses want them to justify political claims, and those who quietly study the djinn-Pact's loopholes risk the binding families' wrath. The circle wants free inquiry under a neutral patron and access to the sealed Pact archives.
