# The Order of the Purifying Flame

The Order of the Purifying Flame is a militant, zealous sub-sect of the [[org:val-dawnfire-church|Order of the Dawnfire]] in [[location:valdris|Valdris]]. While the main church concerns itself with the dragon-bonds and the broader spiritual health of the kingdom, the Purifiers exist to root out heresy, corruption, and the breaking of holy oaths.

## The Inquisition
Clad in stark white tabards marked with a burning sun, the Purifiers operate with chilling autonomy. They are investigators and executioners rolled into one. They hunt down hedge-mages practicing unsanctioned magic, nobles suspected of breaking their dynastic oaths, and anyone caught trafficking with the "uncivilized" spirits of [[location:verdania|Verdania]] or the runic magics of [[location:hrafnland|Hrafnland]].

They believe that the light of the Dawnfire must be kept pure, and that purity requires burning away the rot. Their methods are brutal. Torture—referred to euphemistically as "The Interrogation of the Light"—is standard practice, and confessions extracted under duress are considered legally and spiritually binding.

## Political Terror
Even high-ranking members of the Valdrian haut-cour fear the Purifiers. Because their authority derives directly from the fundamentalist wing of the Cathedral of the Dawnfire, secular lords find it difficult to stop them without being accused of heresy themselves. The Order thrives on this fear, turning neighbors into informants and ensuring that the shadow of the pyre hangs over any whispered dissent against the church.
