# The Red Sands Syndicate

Operating in the lawless stretches of the desert and hiding within the side-gorges of the [[location:border-silk-pass|Silk Pass]], the Red Sands Syndicate is a brutal criminal enterprise based nominally in the territories of [[location:al-khayzar|Al-Khayzar]]. They are not bound by the Pact of the Lamp, nor do they care for the elegant negotiations typical of Al-Khayzari trade.

## Human Trafficking and Smuggling
The Syndicate is heavily involved in human trafficking, kidnapping, and the illegal exploitation of debt-slavery. While debt-slavery and indentured servitude are regulated legal practices within the [[org:chain-markets-of-khalesh|Chain-Markets of Khalesh]], the Syndicate bypasses these laws entirely. They raid unprotected caravans and vulnerable border villages in southern [[location:sangguo|Sangguo]], capturing civilians to sell in underground markets or to force into brutal labor in illegal spice-mining operations deep in the desert.

## Brutality and Control
They maintain control through absolute terror. They do not employ highly trained assassins like the Silent Veil, nor disciplined soldiers like the Blood-Iron Company; they use thugs, disgraced djinn-binders willing to use minor fire-spirits for torture, and desperate bandits who have nowhere else to go.

If a caravan refuses to pay their exorbitant extortion fees, the Syndicate does not just kill the guards—they take the merchants alive, ensuring their screams echo down the canyon walls of the Silk Pass to serve as a warning to the next caravan. They represent the darkest, most predatory aspects of survival in a harsh landscape, completely devoid of honor or regulation.
