The brotherhood of master shipwrights who build the longships at the Drekahöfn shipyards, where Tor Longkeel, Bjorn Keelwright, and Greta Keelson lay the keels of the fleet. The brotherhood is sworn to build only for jarls who honor the old oaths, yet Jarl Erik's raiders offer double pay for war-ships while High Jarl Sigrid wants the fleet for defense, not raids. The brotherhood wants to keep the shipyards independent of any single jarl and preserve the longship-building secrets for the whole folk.
