The cooperative of deep-water fishers who coordinate the fleet, share the catch, and ration the reef through lean seasons, with the deep-water fisher Rangi Deep-Cast, the reef-walker Moana, the net-maker Tui Lash-Maker, and the chief's son Tamatoa among its crews. The cooperative honors the old rule of shared catch, but the growing refugee population strains the ration, and established fishers resent splitting hard-won deep-water hauls with newcomers. The cooperative wants to feed all the atolls without collapsing the reefs and settle how many refugees the fishing grounds can truly bear.
