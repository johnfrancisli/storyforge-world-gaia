The consortium of river-traders and canoe pilots who control traffic on the great rivers and the cascade trade-stairs, counting Amara of the Long Boat, the master pilot Corrente Watersong, the salt-trade leader Suri Salt-Scales, and the message-carrier Bahri Swift-Current among its fleet. The consortium wants to open cautious trade with the outsider metal-people of Dr. Voss's expedition for steel tools, but the shamans and temple guardians warn that foreign goods corrupt the spirit-pacts, and the trader Marcela Good-Trade smuggles outsider wares already. The consortium wants a monopoly on river trade and the profit of careful contact with the outside world.
