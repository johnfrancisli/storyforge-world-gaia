The guild of tattoo-carvers who record lineage, voyages, and rank in the skin of every islander — the living archive of the Archipelago — where master carvers such as Mana the Tattoo-carver inscribe the route-maps that wayfinders carry on their own bodies. The guild guards the sacred patterns, but as refugees arrive carrying their own marking traditions and some carvers tattoo outsiders for coin, the elders fear the patterns' meaning is being diluted and lost. The guild wants to protect the sacred patterns' meaning and decide whether refugees may earn the marks of a new home.
