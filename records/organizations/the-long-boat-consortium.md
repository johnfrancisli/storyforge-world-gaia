The consortium of river-traders and canoe pilots who control traffic on the great rivers and the cascade trade-stairs, counting [[character:amara-of-the-long-boat|Amara of the Long Boat]], the master pilot [[character:corrente-watersong|Corrente Watersong]], the salt-trade leader [[character:suri-salt-scales|Suri Salt-Scales]], and the message-carrier [[character:bahri-swift-current|Bahri Swift-Current]] among its fleet. The consortium wants to open cautious trade with the outsider metal-people of Dr. Voss's expedition for steel tools, but the shamans and temple guardians warn that foreign goods corrupt the spirit-pacts, and the trader [[character:marcela-good-trade|Marcela Good-Trade]] smuggles outsider wares already. The consortium wants a monopoly on river trade and the profit of careful contact with the outside world.
