### Chronicle

On the first dawn of Blossom month in the five hundred and eighty-seventh year, a soul opened its eyes in a garden that had no horizon. The air was warm and smelled of crushed mint and stone wet with spring rain. Floating islands drifted like sleeping beasts through layers of white cloud, and blossoms opened in colors that belonged to no single season on the mortal earth below.

At the center of the grove stood the open pavilion of pale celestial granite. [[character:the-guide|The Guide]] sat upon the stone bench, hands resting upon plain linen knees, watching the newcomer with patient dark eyes. No words of destiny were spoken; no gods claimed the soul's allegiance. The Guide offered only a cup of clear water and pointed downward through the break in the clouds, where the seven nations of Gaia lay spread in emerald valleys, desert sands, and storm-carved fjords.

The choice of where to fall belonged entirely to the traveler.
