### Chronicle

At Highsun in the five hundred and eighty-fourth year, fifty knights of [[org:dragon-order|the Dragon Order]] knelt in the high meadow below [[location:sunhelm-keep|Sunhelm Keep]] to speak the ancient binding-vows before the wyrms roosting on the ridges above. Sunlight glinted off polished steel, the Dawnfire priests raised their consecrated banners, and the solemn oaths of fire and blood were spoken in the old tongue.

No answer came.

Where the great wyrms had once answered with rumbling thunder and mind-to-mind resonance that filled a knight's skull with golden warmth, there was only the sound of high wind through the pines. [[character:aldrich-whitmore|Sir Aldrich]] called silently through the tether to his dragon Verath; nothing answered save a cold, bottomless emptiness. Beside him, [[character:kael-drakesworn|Sir Kael]] gripped his hilt until his gauntlets creaked, feeling the ancient bond wither, while [[character:aurelia-dawnspear|Dame Aurelia]] stared up at the motionless ridges with wide, unbelieving eyes.

When the ceremony ended, the knights stood up, smiled for the watching commoners, and swore to each other that none would speak a word of the silence.
