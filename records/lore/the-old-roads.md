A vanished network of spirit-paths that once connected every nation in Gaia. Walking a working stretch can take you across the world in an afternoon. Most are broken. Some are lost. A few are deliberately kept shut by the people who live near them. Finding a working entrance is valuable. Using one is noticeable — the Roads leave a trace that certain people can follow.
