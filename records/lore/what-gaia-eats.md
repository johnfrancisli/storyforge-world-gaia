Every nation eats what its land will give and what its trade-routes will carry. Tsukuyomi builds meals around rice, river-fish, and pickled vegetables, with the tea ceremony elevated to a form of diplomacy in itself. Valdris runs on rye bread, root vegetables, river-trout, and mead brewed from the highland hives, with a roast reserved for feast-days. Sangguo layers noodles, dumplings, and slow-simmered broths with the silk-road's spices, and its tea houses serve food as deliberately as they serve tea. Verdania's canopy tribes cook over raised hearths — river-fish, jungle fruit, roasted roots, and game taken under the jaguar-pact — and share every meal as a small ceremony of the tribe. Hrafnland lives on smoked fish, preserved meats, flatbread, and fermented dairy through the long winter, with a great feast meaning someone brought back a whale. Al-Khayzar feasts on dates, lamb, flatbreads baked in clay ovens, and the saffron-and-myrrh spiced dishes that the spice houses trade the world for. The Tide Archipelago eats what the reef surrenders — fish, shellfish, taro, breadfruit, coconut — and a feast is an atoll sharing one catch beneath the stars.
