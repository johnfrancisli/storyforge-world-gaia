# The Culture of Hrafnland

The culture of [[location:hrafnland|Hrafnland]] is forged in ice, iron, and the salt spray of the northern seas. It is a pragmatic, rugged society that values strength, loyalty, and the sagas of their ancestors above all else.

## Cultural Themes

*   **Sagas and Ancestry**: History in Hrafnland is not written in books; it is sung in the mead halls. A person's worth is tied directly to the deeds of their ancestors and their own ability to add to the clan's saga.
*   **Strength, Honor, and the Sea**: The sea provides and the sea takes. The culture reveres those who master the waves and those who show unwavering courage in battle. Honor is paramount, and an insult to a clan's honor demands a response.
*   **Shipbuilding and Industry**: The longship is the symbol of Hrafnland. The crafting of ships, weapons, and iron tools is considered sacred work.

## Social Practices

*   **Boasting and Oath-Swearing**: Feasts are the social anchor of Hrafnland. Here, warriors boast of past deeds and swear binding oaths on their weapons to achieve future glory. Failing to fulfill an oath brings deep shame.
*   **Loyalty to the Jarl**: Society is structured around the Jarls. In exchange for protection and a share of the raid-spoil, followers offer absolute loyalty and military service.
*   **Reverence for Craftsmanship**: A master shipwright or smith commands as much respect as a seasoned warrior. The launch of a new longship is a major cultural event, accompanied by elaborate feasts and blessings.

## Atmosphere

Hrafnland is cold, rugged, and profoundly pragmatic. It is a land echoing with the sounds of the forge, the rhythmic striking of the shipyard, and the constant roar of the sea. The air smells of woodsmoke, pine-tar, and the sweat of hard labor.
