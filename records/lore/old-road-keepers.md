name: The Keepers of the Old Roads
context_tier: partial
---

The Old Roads are mostly broken, but the few working entrances that remain are not unattended. Each is watched over by a keeper — sometimes a hereditary family, sometimes a lone hermit, sometimes a small order — who lives near the sealed way and ensures it stays sealed, or opens only on their terms. The keepers do not advertise; most Gaian villagers live within a day's walk of a keeper's cottage and never learn what it guards. The shrine network tends the Tsukuyomi entrances, the Dragon Order unofficially watches the Valdris ones, and in Verdania the temple guardians of the Coiled Serpent hold a sealed Road-gate beneath the pyramid. The keepers share one quiet understanding: a working Old Road is more dangerous than a war, because the people who walk it arrive where no one is watching for them.
