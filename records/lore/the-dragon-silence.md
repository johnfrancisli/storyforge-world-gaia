In Valdris a dragon's oath once meant something unbreakable — a bond sworn wing to sword, sealed in fire and old language, honored unto death. A generation ago the dragons stopped answering. They did not die and they did not leave; the great wyrms still roost in the high peaks, but they will not speak, will not swear, and will not answer the Dragon Order's binding-calls. The knights say the bond-pact was broken by mortal arrogance; the mages of the Veiled Flame whisper that the dragons saw something on the wind and withdrew. Whatever the cause, the Order still trains its knights in the old rites, performing rituals to an audience that no longer speaks back — and the silence itself has become the kingdom's quietest crisis.
