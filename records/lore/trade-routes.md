Gaia's commerce runs on three great arteries and a hundred smaller ones. The Sea-Lane threads the Tide Archipelago to Hrafnland's fjords and Tsukuyomi's harbors, carrying pearls, salt-fish, and amber against iron, furs, and lacquerware. The Silk Road crosses Sangguo's caravanserais and climbs the mountain passes into Valdris, bearing spice and silk toward the northern kingdoms. The Broken Road of Al-Khayzar stitches the desert oasis-cities to the river-stairs of Verdania, where jungle goods meet caravan goods at the cascade markets. Every route pays toll to someone — a jarl, a guild, a binding family, a canopy council — and every route is watched by the people who profit from it and the bandits who'd rather take the cargo than the toll.
