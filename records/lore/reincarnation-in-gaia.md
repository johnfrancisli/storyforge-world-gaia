Occasionally a person arrives in Gaia carrying memories of another life — another world entirely. These souls are rare, perhaps one in a generation. They arrive in the Heavenly Realm, where a guide helps them choose a nation to begin in. They are called god-touched, and the name is both a compliment and a warning. They often grow into remarkable people, and just as often into troubled ones.
