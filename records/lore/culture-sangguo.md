# The Culture of Sangguo

The culture of [[location:sangguo|Sangguo]] is ancient, deliberate, and deeply intertwined with the spiritual order. It is a society that prizes harmony above all, where every action is a reflection on one's ancestors and the celestial bureaucracy.

## Cultural Themes

*   **Harmony and Tradition**: Sangguo believes in a fundamental order to the universe. Disrupting this harmony through rash action or disrespect for tradition is considered the gravest of offenses.
*   **Ancestral Reverence**: The ancestors are not merely dead; they are an active part of the celestial bureaucracy. A family's fortunes are directly tied to how well they honor and appease their forebears.
*   **Courtly Etiquette and Subtlety**: Direct confrontation is avoided. Politics and disagreements are handled through intricate layers of etiquette, where a misplaced word or a subtle metaphor can seal an alliance or declare a war.

## Social Practices

*   **Strict Adherence to Hierarchy**: Society is rigidly structured. Respect for elders and superiors is absolute, and every interaction is governed by strict protocols of address and behavior.
*   **Subtle Communication**: Poetry, calligraphy, and the tea ceremony are not just arts; they are modes of communication. Displeasure or intent is often conveyed indirectly through these mediums.
*   **Daily Rituals**: Maintaining 'face'—one's social standing and dignity—is a daily practice. Rituals honoring the ancestors and the spirits of nature are seamlessly woven into everyday life.

## Atmosphere

Sangguo is formal, measured, and highly stratified. It is a nation steeped in centuries of unbroken tradition, where the atmosphere is one of quiet contemplation, underlying political tension, and breathtaking, manicured beauty.
