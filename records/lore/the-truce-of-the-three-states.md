[[location:sangguo|Sangguo]] is one nation in name and three in fact: Jin in the north, Chu in the south, and Wei wedged between them, each ruled by its own warlord and each claiming the mantle of the old empire. The Last War exhausted all three without settling anything, and the Treaty of Ash and Salt froze their borders in place rather than ending their ambitions. Open battle is rare now, but the rivalry has simply moved indoors — into the Pavilion of the Jade Diagram where strategists scheme, the silk-road bazaars where merchant houses outbid each other, and the tea houses where poison is quieter than a sword. The truce holds because none of the three can afford to break it, not because any of them has stopped wanting to.
