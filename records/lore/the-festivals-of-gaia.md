Every nation marks the turning year with at least one great festival, and a traveler moving across Gaia could find a celebration in nearly every month. The festivals are how the nations remember who they are: Tsukuyomi's moon-viewing and blossom-viewing, Valdris's Dragonfire Vow and Harvestmoon mead-feast, [[location:sangguo|Sangguo]]'s Lantern Night and the Three-States Tea Conclave, [[location:verdania|Verdania]]'s Jaguar Moon and the Serpent-Waking, [[location:hrafnland|Hrafnland]]'s Midwinter Blot and Longship Launch, [[location:al-khayzar|Al-Khayzar]]'s Saffron Harvest and the Night of True Names, and the Tide Archipelago's Pearl Dive and the Rising Tide memorial. Some are joyous, some are solemn, and more than a few are the only day of the year that rival factions will share a table without reaching for a blade.
