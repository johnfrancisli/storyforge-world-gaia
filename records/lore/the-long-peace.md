A generation of relative peace across Gaia, following the last great war between [[location:sangguo|Sangguo]]'s states. The peace is uneven — some nations prosper, others simmer. The treaty that ended the war is fraying, and not everyone remembers why it was signed. The current generation is the first to grow up without war, and some of them do not understand what they would be losing.
