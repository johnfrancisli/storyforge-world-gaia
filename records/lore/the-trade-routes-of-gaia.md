Gaia's commerce runs on three great arteries and a hundred smaller ones. The Sea-Lane threads the Tide Archipelago to [[location:hrafnland|Hrafnland]]'s fjords and Tsukuyomi's harbors, carrying pearls, salt-fish, and amber against iron, furs, and lacquerware. The Silk Road crosses [[location:sangguo|Sangguo]]'s caravanserais and climbs the mountain passes into Valdris, bearing spice and silk toward the northern kingdoms. The Broken Road of [[location:al-khayzar|Al-Khayzar]] stitches the desert oasis-cities to the river-stairs of [[location:verdania|Verdania]], where jungle goods meet caravan goods at the cascade markets. Every route pays toll to someone — a jarl, a guild, a binding family, a canopy council — and every route is watched by the people who profit from it and the bandits who'd rather take the cargo than the toll.
