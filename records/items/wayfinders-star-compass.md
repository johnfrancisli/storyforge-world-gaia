name: Wayfinder's Star Compass
item_type: tool
appearance: A disc of polished pearl-shell the width of an outstretched hand, set with thirty-two points
  marked in tiny drilled holes filled with black pigment and a central pivot of bone. Etched lines radiate
  from the center in the patterns of the great star-paths. The shell catches light and seems to hold a
  second sky within it.
description: A navigation instrument of the Tide Archipelago wayfinder guilds, used alongside the wayfinder's
  staff to fix a canoe's heading by the stars when the horizon is unclear. The compass does not point
  north -- it points to the star-paths, the rising and setting points of the guide-stars a wayfinder has
  memorized, and a master can read position, season, and the lean of the current from the way the etched
  lines catch the night. Each compass is personally calibrated by the wayfinder who carries it and is
  useless in another's hands, the holes filled to their own star-memory. A compass is given by a teacher
  to a student upon completion of their long solo voyage and is, after a staff, the wayfinder's most guarded
  possession.
