name: Desert Navigator's Astrolabe
item_type: tool
appearance: A brass astrolabe the width of two palms, its face a nest of nested rings engraved with star-coordinates
  and the phases of the desert moon. The central alidade is set with a small chip of starlight-glass that
  catches even faint light. The whole is worn smooth by thumbs and hangs from a cord of braided camel-leather.
description: The instrument of a master caravan navigator of Al-Khayzar, who guides trade caravans across
  the deep desert where there are no roads and no landmarks but the stars. A skilled navigator with a
  true astrolabe can fix a position to within a dune-ridge at night and read the stars through the day-star
  for a rough heading. The starlight-glass at its heart is a fragment of the same captured light the Valdris
  elves use in their lanterns, traded across the Old Roads in the days when they still ran -- it lets
  the astrolabe's user take a star-sighting even in the haze of a sandstorm. A navigator's astrolabe is
  their livelihood and their honor; losing one in the deep desert is a death sentence, and one recovered
  from the sands is returned to its navigator's family with great ceremony.
