name: Shrine Bell of Silence
item_type: religious
appearance: A bronze bell the size of a closed fist, hung from a cord of braided red and white cotton.
  The metal is dark with age and etched with a ring of tiny shrine characters that no living kannushi
  can fully read. It rings with a clear, thin note that carries further than it should.
description: A ritual bell carried by senior shrine keepers on the night rounds of the ward network. When
  rung with the proper intent, the bell's note silences yokai within earshot -- not by banishing them,
  but by quieting the part of them that speaks, howls, and draws attention. A silenced yokai cannot call
  for others, cannot lay a curse, and cannot be heard by the spirits it serves. The bells are consecrated
  at the head shrine in Tsuki-no-Miyako and only loaned, never given; a keeper who loses one must report
  to the capital and explain themselves to the head miko. There are perhaps forty in existence.
