appearance: A smooth river-stone, flat and palm-sized, dark green with a single pale vein running through
  it like a dried watercourse. When placed in still water the vein darkens and shifts, and faint lines
  appear on the stone's surface that were not visible dry. The stone is cool and slightly damp to the
  touch no matter the weather.
description: A divination stone used by the senior shamans of the Verdania river cities. Cast into a bowl
  of river water, the oracle shows the pattern of the current around it, and a trained reader can interpret
  the eddies and lines as a reading of what the river knows -- which travelers are upstream, which storms
  are coming, which spirits are moving. The oracle only answers questions about things the water has touched
  or will touch, which limits it more than outsiders assume. The most powerful oracles are stones taken
  from the confluence of three rivers, and the temple-city shamans of the Green Cathedral claim theirs
  is from the place where every river in Verdania once met. They do not let outsiders see it.
