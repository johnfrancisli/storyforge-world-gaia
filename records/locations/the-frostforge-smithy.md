In a stone-walled yard behind the quay, where the salt wind is broken by a screen of driftwood and whalebone, the Frostforge Smithy is [[location:skaldvik|Skaldvik]]'s premier iron-works—a single, soot-blackened forge manned by a family of smiths who have worked the same anvil for six generations and who claim their first ancestor was taught by a dwarf in exchange for a night's hospitality. The smithy is open to the weather on three sides, its roof of turf and timber smoking with the heat of a furnace fed by charcoal shipped from the inland forests, and the ring of the master smith's hammer on the anvil is a sound the whole harbor knows. The smiths of the Frostforge work bog iron and southern bar-iron into the nails, rivets, axes, and ship-fittings that keep [[location:hrafnland|Hrafnland]]'s fleet afloat, and their finest work—decorated axe-heads, inlaid seax-blades, and the rare iron war-helm—commands prices that only a jarl can pay. The master smith, a broad, burned woman named Ragnhild Coal-Hand, is said to know the secret of quenching steel in saltwater to give it a frost-blue edge, and she guards the secret as closely as a rune-carver guards his marks. The smithy smells of hot iron, coal smoke, and the sea, and its sparks fly out into the night like a constant, small constellation.
