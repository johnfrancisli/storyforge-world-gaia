The oldest shrine in [[location:tsu-kawazaki|Kawazaki]], set on a rise above the warehouse district where the harbor wardstones are anchored. A modest building — unpainted cedar, a mossy torii, paper lanterns replaced more often than the shrine keeper would like. The real structure is below ground: a chamber where the ward network's anchor stones pulse with slow blue light, maintained by a lineage of shrine keepers who have served since before the Ashikara came to power. The keeper, an old woman named Ume-no-ji, can read the wards the way a physician reads a pulse. When the fog brings something hungry, the shrine is the first to know. Pilgrims come here to ask safe passage; sailors come to leave offerings. Both are listened to, in different ways.
