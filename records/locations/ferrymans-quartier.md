A riverside neighborhood of boatyards, ferry houses, and net-drying racks on the south bank where the Silver Serpent widens before the confluence, the Ferryman's Quartier is home to Sanyuan's boatmen, ferrymen, and fisher families—a close-knit community bound by the water and suspicious of outsiders. Wooden walkways cross the shallows between houses built on stilts above the flood line, and every home has a boat dock where the family's sampan is tied. The smell is of river water, fish, hemp rope, and the pine tar used to seal hulls. The boatmen's guild hall sits at the district's center, a weathered timber building where ferry routes are assigned, tolls are divided, and disputes between boatmen are settled by the guild elder—a position that has passed through one family for seven generations.
