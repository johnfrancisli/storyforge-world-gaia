The district below the castle where the smiths work, built along the river for the water wheels that drive the bellows. The air tastes of charcoal and hot iron; the sound of hammers is constant, a rhythm the town sleeps to. The master smiths are Kagami vassals by blood, and their forging secrets are passed father to son with an oath that is said to bind even after death. The finest blades in Tsukuyomi come out of these forges — folded steel with a hamon like frozen moonlight on water. A blade from the Forge Quarter is a status symbol in the capital, and a practical one on the battlefield. The smiths will not sell to the Ashikara directly. The blades reach the capital through intermediaries, at a markup, and the Kagami pretend not to notice.
