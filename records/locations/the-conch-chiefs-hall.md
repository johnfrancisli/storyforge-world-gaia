The Conch-Chief's Hall is the seat of [[location:lani-atoll|Lani Atoll]]'s governance and the meeting-place of the Tide-Moot, the great council of the archipelago's island-chiefs, built on the atoll's highest ground — a great mound of sand and coral-stone raised by generations of chiefs to lift the hall above the highest storm-tide. The hall is a great open-sided building of hardwood and palm-thatch, its roof sweeping low at the eaves and rising to a high ridge-pole carved with the coiled forms of octopus and sea-serpent and painted in black, red, and white, its pillars carved with the figures of past chiefs and hung with the great conch-shell trumpets that give the hall its name. The Tide-Moot meets here at the turn of every season, the island-chiefs and elders gathering from across the archipelago to decide the affairs of the nation — war and peace, the allotment of fishing-grounds, the aid due to islands threatened by the rising seas, the disputes that cannot be settled locally — and the Conch-Chief of [[location:lani-atoll|Lani Atoll]], the first among equals, presides from a raised seat of polished coral-stone draped with tapa-cloth, the speaking-staff of carved whale-bone in hand. The floor is sand covered with fine woven mats, and the walls are hung with the tribute and record of the archipelago — feather-work, obsidian blades, shell-wealth, and the bark-paper tallies of every Moot going back generations. The air smells of salt, palm, woodsmoke from the central hearth, and the kava-root brew that is served to every chief at Moot. A great conch hangs by the Conch-Chief's seat, blown only to open and close the Moot.
