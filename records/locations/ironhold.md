Hewn from the living granite of the Frostvein Mountains, the dwarven mountain-hold of Ironhold is less a city than a cathedral of stone—a vast subterranean complex of halls, forges, and galleries that descends seven levels into the earth. The Great Gate, a slab of iron-banded granite twenty feet thick, opens onto a trade-road that winds down into a valley of slag and snow, but beyond it lies a world of vaulted ceilings supported by pillars carved into the shapes of dwarven heroes, lit by basins of eternal forge-fire that cast everything in a warm copper glow. The air is thick with the clang of hammers, the hiss of quenching steel, and the deep, resonant chanting of the Forge-Priests who tend the Everflame. The smell of hot metal, coal smoke, and pickled cave-fish saturates every stone. Dwarves in soot-stained leather aprons trundle ore carts along rails cut into the floor, while children play among the ancestors' statues and elders debate lineage in the mead halls over cups of black stout. Ironhold supplies the kingdom's finest steel and its deepest grudges, and its Thane answers to [[location:crownhaven|Crownhaven]] with a loyalty that is loud, conditional, and grudgingly genuine.
