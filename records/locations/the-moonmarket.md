Where Elfshade's main path widens into a mossy glade beneath a gap in the canopy that admits moonlight and starlight, the Moonmarket opens each night as the sun sets and closes at the first grey light of dawn—a market that trades in darkness and operates by rules no human merchant would recognize. There are no stalls or coin here; instead, elven artisans spread their wares on mossy stones and fallen logs—enchanted jewelry that glows with captured starlight, vials of moon-dew gathered from the highest branches, thornwhisper silk that shimmers like a soap bubble, and song-charms that replay a melody when broken. Trade is conducted by barter, by promise, or by the exchange of memories, which the elves can extract and preserve in crystal phials. The market is lit only by the starlight lanterns and the glow of the wares themselves, and the effect is of walking through a field of fallen stars. The air smells of jasmine, wet moss, and the sharp, electric scent of active enchantments. Human visitors are welcome but often leave having traded away something they cannot quite name, and the elves, for their part, are always delighted by the strange and impractical things humans will offer for a pretty light.
