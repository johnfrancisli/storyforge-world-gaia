The Stone Seat is the seat of the Conclave of Gem-Houses and the Foreman of Naqd, a fortress-palace of black iron-stained sandstone at the defile's highest point, its walls buttressed against the cliff and its highest tower carved from the living rock so that it seems to grow out of the mountain. Within, the great hall is a long chamber lit by high narrow windows, its floor of polished stone inlaid with a mosaic of turquoise and carnelian, its walls hung with the banners of the five Gem-Houses and the great maps of the mountain's veins that the mine-surveyors keep. The Foreman's seat is a chair cut from a single block of dark lapis, set on a dais at the hall's end, and around it the five House-Masters sit on lower stone benches when the Conclave is in session. The hall is cool and echoes, and the air smells of stone-dust, lamp-oil, and the faint metallic tang of the deep mines. The Conclave meets here to divide claims, settle boundaries, and judge mine-crimes, and the Foreman's rulings are recorded by scribes on tablets of smooth stone. Guards in iron-stained leather with pick-axes rather than swords stand at the doors.
