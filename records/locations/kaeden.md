A castle town in the western foothills held by the Kagami clan, the Ashikara's oldest rival. The castle itself sits on a steep ridge above the river — black-timbered, iron-roofed, built to be held against a shogun. The town below has grown rich and sullen in its shadow. Forges ring day and night; the Kagami have been the finest swordsmiths in Tsukuyomi for five generations, and their steel is the one thing the Ashikara cannot match. The streets are wider than the capital's and better drained, the houses sturdier, the samurai district nearly as large as the merchant quarter. There is a calm here that is the calm of a place that knows its own strength and is waiting. Daimyo Kagami Renji has not attended the shogun's court in three years. His replies to summons are courteous, and empty.
