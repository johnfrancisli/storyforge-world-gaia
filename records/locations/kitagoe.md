A fortress town at the northern edge of Tsukuyomi, where the Cedar Road meets the Frostspine passes and the wild yokai come down out of the deep mountains when the snows drive them. The town is built for defense — a stockade of iron-banded cedar, a central keep on a granite knob, narrow streets that funnel an attacker into killing grounds. The garrison flies the Ashikara mon but the soldiers are a hard mix of clan retainers, ronin, and mountain folk who have fought the yokai all their lives. The cold is the real ruler here. Frost rims the eaves eight months of the year, and the ward fires burn blue at the gates. The town smells of pine resin, smoke, and the iron tang of the forge that never stops, because a broken wardstone in Kitagoe is not an inconvenience — it is a death sentence for someone. Two thousand people live here, and most of them can use a weapon. They do not grow rice. They grow survivors.
