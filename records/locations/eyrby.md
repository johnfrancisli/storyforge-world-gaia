Nestled in the sheltered crook of the Sølvfjord where the winter winds lose their teeth and the long summer light turns the meadows gold, Eyrby is the southern breadbasket of [[location:hrafnland|Hrafnland]]—a farming town of turf-roofed longhouses, stone-walled fields, and barley terraces carved into the fjord's gentle slopes, where the air smells of cut hay, woodsmoke, and the sweet tang of fermenting malt. The town stretches along a pebbled shore where fishing boats bob beside irrigation ditches, and goats and cattle wander lanes fenced with driftwood and whalebone. Its people are stocky, sun-browned, and uncommonly content, their cheeks red from labor and mead, and they speak with the unhurried drawl of folk who measure time by the harvest moon rather than the jarl's horn. Windbreaks of ancient birch and rowan, scarred with old harvest-runes, ring the settlement, and the drone of bees from the great mead-orchards is the town's constant music. Eyrby feeds [[location:skaldvik|Skaldvik]] and the fighting men of the north with grain, malt, and smoked meat, and its storehouses—thick-walled, thatched, and half-buried against the frost—hold enough to see [[location:hrafnland|Hrafnland]] through the harshest winter the frost giants have ever sent.
