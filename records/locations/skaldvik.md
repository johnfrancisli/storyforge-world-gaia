The largest trading port in [[location:hrafnland|Hrafnland]]. A fjord town of longhouses and warehouses, shipyards and mead halls, built where the fjord meets the open sea. Longships line the dock, carrying northern furs and foreign cargoes. At the dueling ring outside the Althing mound, the imposing demon challenger [[character:morvath-the-unbeaten|Morvath the Unbeaten]] tests the iron resolve of hearthguards like [[character:torsten-brokenshield|Torsten Brokenshield]], granting embers of demon strength to those who withstand his blows. In the harbor smithies, visiting Tsukuyomi master swordsmith [[character:takahashi-nobu|Takahashi Nobu]] works at the anvil alongside [[character:ragna-forgebreak|Ragna Forgebreak]], combining clay-folded steel with cold bog-iron.
