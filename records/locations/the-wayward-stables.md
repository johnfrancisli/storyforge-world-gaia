Occupying a full block on the northern edge of town, a complex of low timber barns, fenced paddocks, and a farrier's forge that together form the largest horse and mule market between [[location:val-rivenspire|Rivenspire]] and the northern frontier, the Wayward Stables are where the trade caravans of [[location:val-crossford|Crossford]] are built, maintained, and rebuilt. Hundreds of horses and mules are stabled, sold, rented, and exchanged here at any given time, and the smell of hay, horse sweat, manure, and the hot iron of the farrier's forge saturates the entire block. Teamsters haggle with horse-traders over the price of a draft mare, while grooms rub down animals fresh off the road and the farrier's hammer rings in a steady, percussive rhythm that carries across the town. The stables' owner, a broad, calloused woman named Mara Bridleborn, is a former cavalry farrier who can judge a horse's worth from a hundred paces and who has never been cheated in a trade—a reputation that makes her both respected and slightly feared by the horse-traders who work her block. The Wayward Stables also board caravans' animals between runs, shoe them, treat their injuries, and, when necessary, put them down with a quiet mercy that Mara insists on performing herself. Without the stables, the trade that flows through [[location:val-crossford|Crossford]] would grind to a halt within a week, and Mara knows it.
