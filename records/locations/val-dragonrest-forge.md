Beneath Dragonrest, in a cavern heated by a natural volcanic vent, the Emberforge burns with a fire that has not been extinguished in four hundred years—a furnace of dwarven design tended by a brotherhood of smiths who are equal parts artisans, priests, and soldiers. The cavern is a cathedral of fire and metal: massive anvils sit on stone platforms around the central furnace, and the walls are hung with finished blades, pikes, and plate armor that gleam in the ember-light. The heat is staggering, the air shimmering and thick with the smell of coal, hot iron, and the acrid bite of quenching oil. The forge-smiths work in leather aprons and nothing else, their bare arms glistening, and they speak little, communicating through the rhythm of their hammers—a language said to be understood only by the steel. The Emberforge produces the finest weapons in Dragonrest's arsenal, including the signature black-steel blades of the Wyrmstone Sentinels, and its master smith, a grizzled dwarf named Brogar Ashhand, is rumored to know the secret of forging dragonbone into steel—a secret he will take to his grave.
