Perched on a stone promontory at the downriver end of the Greyfen Quays, the Harbor Watch Keep is a squat, iron-shuttered tower that serves as the headquarters of the [[location:val-rivenspire|Rivenspire]] City Watch's harbor division—a force of sixty men and six boats tasked with policing the docks, the river traffic, and the smugglers' tunnels beneath the quays. The keep is functional, not beautiful: its walls are stained with river spray, its iron shutters are dented from objects thrown by angry crowds, and its interior smells of wet wool, lamp oil, and the sour residue of the holding cells. The harbor commander, a weathered, one-legged woman named Commander Vella Drake, runs her division with a combination of ruthless efficiency and grudging respect for the dockside culture she polices. A war-room on the keep's second floor holds a map of the quay tunnels, painstakingly compiled from informants' testimony and the confessions of captured smugglers—though the map is admitted to be only half complete. The keep's signal tower can raise alarm lights visible from the Riven Bridge, and its boats patrol the river from dusk to dawn, but every officer knows that for every smuggler they catch, ten more slip through in the dark.
