The Shelter Rows are the residential heart of [[location:tide-motu-lotu|Motu Lotu]], the dense warren of driftwood-and-thatch shelters that stretches along the crescent beach and inland among the palms, each shelter a small compound of one or two sleeping-huts and a cook-space, walled with woven palm and patched with sail-cloth, bark-cloth, and whatever a family has salvaged or bartered. The lanes between them are sand, swept clean by each household and marked with shells and stones to show the boundary, and the rows are organized loosely by clan-community, each cluster flying the signal-cloth of its home-island from a high pole so that a new arrival can find their own people. The shelters are built close for wind-shelter and for the comfort of neighbors, and the sound of the rows is of children playing, women talking across the lane-walls, the rhythmic thud of breadfruit being pounded, and the low chanting of elders teaching the old songs to children who will never see the islands they describe. The smell is of cook-smoke, thatch, salt, and the sweet rot of palm-husks composting in the small garden-patches where each household grows a few taro and sweet-potato in the poor sand, supplemented by the fish caught from the reef. A lane-elder, chosen by each cluster, keeps the peace and reports needs to the Camp-Council, and the rows are rebuilt and extended constantly as new arrivals come and old shelters rot in the salt air.
