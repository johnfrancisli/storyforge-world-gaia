The Salt Market is the great market of [[location:ver-tidalreach|Tidal Reach]], a sprawling complex of floating platforms and stilt-walkways at the city's centre where the morning's catch is landed and traded before the heat of the day. Fish of every kind lie on woven mats and palm-leaf tables — silver river-fish, flat skates, armoured catfish, great tunas from the open sea, strings of sardines, and the prized river-dolkins — alongside baskets of clams and oysters, crabs bound with twine, octopus, and slabs of sea-salt dried in shallow clay pans. The smell is overwhelming — fish, brine, the iron tang of blood on the gutting-tables, and the sharp reek of the fermented fish-paste the city exports inland. Fishmongers shout prices and provenance, buyers from the river barges haggle in rapid hand-signs, and gulls scream and dive for scraps, so bold they steal from the stalls. Beyond the fish, the market sells salt, dugong oil in clay jars, mother-of-pearl inlay work, sails and rope, and the bitter dark chocolate that comes overland from [[location:ver-cascadeexchange|Cascade Exchange]] in exchange for the sea's bounty.
