Straddling the great Greyfen River at the place where it narrows to a thundering gorge before spilling toward the southern sea, Rivenspire is the beating commercial heart of Valdris—a city of waterwheels, warehouses, and smoke-stained brick that never truly sleeps. Stone quays line both banks, groaning under crates of grain, ore, and cloth, while bargemen shout commands in a dozen dialects over the roar of the current. The city's namesake spire is a lone limestone column rising from the river's center, crowned by a lighthouse whose amber beacon guides night traffic through the treacherous gorge. The smell of river mud, tar, and fresh-baked bread from the bakers' guild mingles with the metallic tang of the smithies that cluster near the waterwheels. Humans, halflings, and dwarves rub shoulders on the crowded dockside lanes, and coin changes hands faster than anywhere else in the kingdom. Brothels and counting houses stand side by side, and the City Watch struggles to keep the river pirates and smugglers honest. To stand on the Riven Bridge at dawn and watch the fog burn off the gorge is to understand why merchants say Rivenspire is where Valdris makes its living.
