A quiet, luminous place above the clouds. Floating islands draped in mist, gardens that bloom in every season at once, and a pavilion at the center that looks down through layers of cloud to the world below. From the pavilion you can see all seven nations spread out like a map. The air is warm without being hot, still without being stale. It is mostly empty, and that emptiness is intentional — this is a place of choosing, not of living.
