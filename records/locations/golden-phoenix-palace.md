The seat of the Jin warlord's power and the heart of Jin-an, the Golden Phoenix Palace is a vast complex of audience halls, private residences, and administrative offices enclosed within a perimeter wall of white stone. The great audience hall at its center features a throne of gilded wood beneath a ceiling painted with a phoenix in flight, its wings spanning the entire dome in gold and vermillion. Silk screens partition private chambers where the warlord's family resides, and the gardens behind the residential quarters contain pavilions for poetry, music, and private council. The palace is staffed by eunuchs, serving women, and a corps of scholar-officials who manage the bureaucracy of the entire Jin state from offices within the compound walls. The air smells of sandalwood incense, fresh ink, and the polished wood of a thousand desks.
