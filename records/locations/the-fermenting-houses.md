The Fermenting Houses are a cluster of large stilt-buildings set apart on the upstream edge of [[location:iara|Iara]], downwind of the chief's float, where the city's staple drink — cassava beer, sour and lightly alcoholic — is produced in great clay vats. The buildings are open-sided to let the air through, their roofs high and thatched to shade the vats, and each is tended by a household of brewers who chew the cassava to start the fermentation, a sacred and slightly shameful labour performed only by senior women. The smell is overwhelming — sweet, sour, yeasty, and warm, the thick heady scent of fermentation that hangs over the whole upstream end of the city and tells every nose which way the wind blows. Great gourds and clay jars of the finished beer are loaded onto canoes and distributed to the market, the chief's hall, and every feast and ritual in the city. Beside the beer-houses, a smaller building ferments palm-wine from the fruit brought down from the canopy, and another produces the honey-mead traded from the bee-keepers of the interior. The brew-mistress, a formidable old woman, controls every batch and her blessing is sought before any beer is served at the chief's hall.
