At the center of Northwatch, surrounded by its double ring of palisade walls, Frosthelm Keep is a squat, grey-granite fortress that has been rebuilt so many times that no two walls are quite the same age—a patchwork of old stone and newer stone, each layer a record of a siege survived or a fire recovered from. The keep is four stories of functional, thick-walled survival, its windows narrow slits, its roof steep-pitched to shed snow, and its central tower crowned with a signal brazier that can be lit to call reinforcements from the nearest garrison three days' ride south. Inside, the walls are hung with weapons, shields, and the tattered banners of companies that have served and died here, and the air smells of pine smoke, tallow, old leather, and the ever-present damp that no fire can fully drive from the stone. The Warden's quarters and war-room occupy the top floor, where a map of the Pale Reach is pinned with markers showing reported goblin and frost-wight activity, and the Knight-Commander's quarters are on the third, spartan and unadorned. The ground floor is a mustering hall and armory, always stocked and always ready, for in Northwatch the next attack is never a matter of if but when.
