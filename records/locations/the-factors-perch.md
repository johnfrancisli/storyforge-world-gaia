The Factor's Perch is the seat of governance in Cascade Exchange, a fortified house of black basalt and ironwood built on the highest terrace overlooking the falls, its back wall the living cliff and its front a long gallery of arched windows open to the spray and the roar. Inside, the floors are polished basalt flagstones worn smooth by generations of boots, and the walls are hung with trade-maps, the painted shields of the trading houses, and a great iron bell said to have been traded from the western kingdoms centuries ago and rung only to warn of attack or flood. The Falls Factor's desk sits at the gallery's end, piled with ledgers, duty-tallies, and sealed bark-paper letters from Iara. Guards in green sashes stand at the door and a clerk is always present to record rulings. The air smells of damp stone, ink, and the strong coffee the Factor drinks constantly. From the gallery you can see the whole stair-market below, the lower docks, and the river stretching away into the green corridor of the jungle.
