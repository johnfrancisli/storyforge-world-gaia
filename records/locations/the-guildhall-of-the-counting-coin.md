The Guildhall of the Counting Coin is the seat of the Merchant Guild of [[location:val-rivenspire|Rivenspire]]—a fortress of commerce that occupies an entire city block near the Riven Bridge and is worth more than most noble estates. Its facade is a restrained grey stone, but within, the guildhall is a cathedral of ledgers and coin: a great hall whose vaulted ceiling is gilded with actual gold leaf, whose walls are hung with tapestries depicting the guild's founding and triumphs, and whose floor is a mosaic of the trade routes of Gaia. Counting rooms, strong-rooms, and arbitration chambers branch off the main hall, and the air smells of ink, candle wax, old leather, and the metallic whisper of coin being counted. The Guildmaster, a shrewd, heavyset woman named Sera Coldcoin, holds court from a raised desk at the hall's end, adjudicating disputes between merchants with a speed and finality that makes the royal courts look glacial. Armed guild-bailiffs in the Guild's blue-and-silver livery guard the strong-rooms, and a private vault beneath the hall is said to hold more gold than the Crown's treasury. The Guildhall is where the real power of [[location:val-rivenspire|Rivenspire]] resides, and every merchant in the city knows that a word from Sera is worth more than a decree from the King.
