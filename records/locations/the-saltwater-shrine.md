The Saltwater Shrine is the spirit-lodge of [[location:ver-tidalreach|Tidal Reach]], built on the oldest platform in the city, a single great raft of dark seasoned timber said to have been lashed together by the city's founders and replaced plank by plank ever since so that it is both ancient and new. The shrine is open to the weather, roofed only by a sail-cloth awning, and its central feature is a tidal pool — a square basin cut into the raft that fills and empties with the sea through a channel, so that the water within is always half river and half sea and changes with every tide. The sea-shaman, a weathered woman with ritual scars down her arms, reads the tides and the moon here, casts offerings of fish-blood and flowers into the pool, and interprets the movements of the small creatures that live in it — a crab, an eel, a blind cave-fish — as messages from the sea-spirit and the river-spirit, whose meeting at the river-mouth is the city's sacred fact. Driftwood charms, shells strung on cord, and the carved figureheads of lost ships hang from the awning-poles and clatter in the wind. The air smells of salt, dried seaweed, and the faint sweetness of the palm-wine offerings poured into the pool.
