The mountain fortress-capital of the Wei state clings to the granite flanks of the Dragon's Spine range like an eagle's nest, a city of black stone walls and iron gates that has never fallen to siege. Cold winds rake through narrow streets cut into the rock, carrying the smell of coal smoke, hot iron from the forges, and the pine resin used to waterproof every surface. Wei banners of slate-grey and blood-red hang limp in the thin air, and at every overlook, sentries watch the western passes with eyes trained since childhood. Below the city, terraced fields of hardy millet and buckwheat cling to the slopes, feeding a population hardened by altitude, scarcity, and the eternal expectation of war. Weidu is not a city of comfort—it is a city of endurance, and its people are proud of it.
