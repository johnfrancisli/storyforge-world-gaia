A vast walled courtyard at the western edge of [[location:san-silkroad-city|Yunmeng]], the Great Caravanserai is the staging ground for every caravan entering or leaving the city—a square of packed earth big enough to hold three hundred camels and their handlers, surrounded by two-story arcades of storerooms, sleeping quarters, and stables. At any hour, the yard is a chaos of loading and unloading: camel drivers shouting in distant tongues, porters carrying crates of gems and dye, and merchants arguing over weights and measures in a babel of languages. The arcades smell of hay, animal musk, and the strange spices leaking from poorly packed crates. A well at the center provides water for men and beasts alike, and a shrine to every major deity—local and foreign—stands in one corner, so travelers may pray for safe passage before departing.
