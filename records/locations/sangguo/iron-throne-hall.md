Carved directly into the mountainside at [[location:san-weidu|Weidu]]'s summit, the Iron Throne Hall is a vast cavern-chamber where natural columns of living rock alternate with forged iron pillars to support a ceiling lost in shadow. The warlord's seat—a chair hammered from the weapons of defeated enemies—sits on a dais of black marble above a fire pit that burns year-round, filling the hall with heat and the acrid smell of coal and old blood. The walls are hung with battle standards taken from every campaign, and the floor is worn smooth by the boots of generations of soldiers who have knelt here. The military council convenes at a crescent table of ironwood, debating strategy over maps pinned with enemy positions while the warlord listens in silence before rendering judgment.
