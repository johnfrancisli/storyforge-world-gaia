A complex of massive storehouses on the east bank of the confluence, the Imperial Granaries of [[location:san-river-junction|Sanyuan]] are the largest grain repository in [[location:sangguo|Sangguo]]—stone-walled, tile-roofed buildings each the size of a city block, raised on stone platforms to resist flood and filled with rice and millet sufficient to feed a city for years. The air inside is dry and dusty, thick with the scent of stored grain and the faint mustiness of old harvests. Grain clerks with abacuses and tally sticks track every bushel in and out, and armed guards patrol the perimeter night and day, for the granaries are a strategic prize that every state covets. Rats are a constant battle, and the granary employs a staff of cat-handlers whose felines are as valued as the soldiers. Barges load and unload at the adjacent wharf in a steady, measured rhythm.
