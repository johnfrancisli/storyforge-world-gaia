The Spirit Nest is the shaman's lodge of [[location:ver-altacanopy|Alta Canopy]], set apart on the highest platform of the central tree, where the canopy thins and the sky is visible. It is a round hut of woven branches daubed with red and black river clay, its walls hung with dream-catchers of bone and feather, rattles made from dried seed-pods, and the preserved skins of jaguars and anacondas given to the lodge as offerings. A low fire burns in the centre on a bed of river sand, its smoke guided out through a hole in the thatch, and the air is thick with the smell of mapacho tobacco, burning copal, and the bitter tang of vision-vines soaking in clay pots. The shaman, an old woman whose face is painted in spirals of black and red, conducts spirit-pacts here — binding a citizen's spirit to that of a totem beast through nights of fasting, chanting, and the drinking of the bitter vine-brew. Spirit drums hang from the rafters and are played only during rituals, their low throbbing said to be audible to the dead. Few come here except at need, and fewer still linger.
