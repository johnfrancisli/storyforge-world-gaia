Straddling the narrow canyon where the Long Pass cuts through the hills between Jin and Wei territory, Longpass Town is a settlement built for war and sustained by the traffic that war brings. The town is split down the center by the canyon road—one half faces east toward Jin, the other west toward Wei—and the tension between the two sides is as constant as the wind that funnels through the pass. Timber buildings with slate roofs huddle behind a timber palisade, and the streets smell of horse sweat, cheap wine, and the coal smoke of smiths who repair armor for whichever side is currently paying. Mercenaries, deserters, spies, and refugees all pass through Longpass, and the town's innkeepers, fences, and informants have grown rich on the spoils of three-way conflict. It is a place where loyalty is fluid and survival is the only principle.
