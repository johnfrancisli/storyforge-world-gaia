A muddy complex of paddocks, barns, and farrier shops on the southern edge of Longpass Town, the Pass Stables breed and trade the hardy mountain horses that the region is known for. The smell of hay, horse dung, and hot horseshoes fills the air, and the sound is a constant mix of whinnying, hammering, and the low calls of stablehands coaxing stubborn mounts. Horse traders from all three states come here to buy warhorses, pack animals, and the swift courier-riders that carry messages across Sangguo's battle lines. The stable-master, a weathered woman named Liao Fen, keeps meticulous breeding records and is said to know each horse's lineage better than most nobles know their own family trees.
