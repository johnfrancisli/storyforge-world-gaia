Atop the highest hill in [[location:crownhaven|Crownhaven]], visible for miles across the plains, Sunhelm Keep rises in seven tiers of white limestone and gilded slate—a fortress-palace that is as much a statement of royal power as it is a working seat of government. The outer walls bristle with ballistae and fly the golden dragon banner of Valdris from every tower, but within, the keep opens into sunlit courtyards, marble galleries, and a great throne hall whose vaulted ceiling is painted with the deeds of the kings. Armored Knights of the Crown patrol the corridors in pairs, their crimson cloaks sweeping the flagstones, while scribes, heralds, and petitioners flow through the lower halls in a controlled current of bureaucracy. The air carries wax, old parchment, and the faint perfume of the royal gardens that terrace down the hill's southern face. The King's own guard, the Sunhelms, are quartered in the upper tiers, and their barracks ring with the clash of practice swords from dawn until dusk. Sunhelm Keep has never fallen to siege, and its walls are said to have been blessed by three separate archmages—a claim no enemy has yet dared to test.
