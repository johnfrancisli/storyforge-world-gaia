A long, low building of pine logs near the north gate, home to the mountain-folk scouts who are the garrison's eyes in the deep forest. The hall smells of pine resin, damp wool, and the musk of something the scouts will not name. The scouts are not clan retainers; they are hunters, trappers, and hermits who know the Frostspine the way a fisherman knows the sea. They track yokai movements, mark safe paths through the snow, and occasionally bring back things — pelts, bones, a wardstone shard found in a place no wardstone should be. The hall's walls are hung with maps drawn on birch bark, updated after every patrol. The scout chief, a weathered woman named Beni, has a working arrangement with a tengu who nests in the upper peaks; the details are private, and no one has survived asking twice.
