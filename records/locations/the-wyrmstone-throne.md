At the peak of Dragonrest's mountain fortress, accessible only by a spiraling staircase cut into the cliff face and defended by three murder-holes and a portcullis of solid iron, sits the Wyrmstone Throne—the seat of the Warden of Dragonrest and the command center from which the mountain fortress is directed. The throne chamber is a natural cavern expanded by dwarven masons into a vaulted hall of black basalt, its walls veined with a strange green mineral that glows faintly in the dark. The throne itself is carved from a single block of green-veined stone said to have been a dragon's resting place, and it is from this that the fortress takes its name. The air is cold and mineral, carrying the distant rumble of subterranean rivers. Maps of the mountain passes cover every table, and a war-council is perpetually in session, for Dragonrest guards the kingdom's most vulnerable highland approach. The Warden's personal guard, the Wyrmstone Sentinels, stand at eternal attention in armor of black steel, and their stillness is so complete that visitors sometimes mistake them for statues until one shifts his weight and the armor whispers against itself.
