The only surface-level entrance to [[location:val-ironhold|Ironhold]] opens onto the Slag Valley—a barren, snow-swept defile between the Frostvein peaks where centuries of mining have stained the rock black and the ground is a crust of slag and cinders that never fully freezes. The trade-road that winds down from the Great Gate to the valley floor is wide enough for a mule-cart but steep, switch-backed, and flanked by watchtowers at every turn, from which the dwarves can rain down rock and arrow on any approaching force. At the valley's mouth, where the defile opens onto the highland meadows, a trading post called Slagpost has grown—a ramshackle cluster of timber buildings where surface-dwelling merchants come to trade with the dwarves without entering the mountain itself. The air in the valley smells of sulfur, cold stone, and coal smoke, and the wind is a constant, keening presence that cuts through fur and wool alike. Dwarven caravan-guards, grim-faced and heavily armed, escort every wagon of ore and steel that descends the road, and the merchants of Slagpost know that trade with [[location:val-ironhold|Ironhold]] is profitable but not for the faint-hearted—the dwarves drive a hard bargain and a harder escort fee, and the mountain roads are not safe for the unescorted.
