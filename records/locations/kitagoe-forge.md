The town forge, which never goes cold. A stone building near the keep with two hearths, a charcoal bin that is refilled daily, and a smith named Goro who is the third generation of his family to hold the position. The forge turns out wardstone casings, gate hardware, arrowheads, and the heavy shortswords the garrison prefers for close work against things that do not stop when a human would. Goro also repairs wardstone brackets — the iron bands that hold the stones in place at the gates and along the stockade — and he has a reputation for being able to reseat a cracked stone well enough to hold for one more winter. The forge is loud, hot, and the one place in Kitagoe that feels like it is winning. Sparks fly into the snow and die hissing.
