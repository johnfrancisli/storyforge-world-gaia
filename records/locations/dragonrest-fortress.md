The mountain fortress of the dragon-bonded knightly order, built into the peaks above [[location:crownhaven|Crownhaven]]. Stone walls scarred by dragonfire, a courtyard wide enough for a dragon to land, and a hall where the bonded knights once gathered. The hall is half-empty now. The old knights maintain the traditions; the younger ones question them. The dragons roost higher in the mountains and do not come down.
