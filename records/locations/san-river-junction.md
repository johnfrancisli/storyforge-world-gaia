At the confluence of the Silver Serpent River flowing south from Jin and the White Crane River descending from the Wei highlands, Sanyuan is a city defined by water—its three districts each occupy the land between branching rivers, connected by bridges of stone and timber that are the city's pride and its vulnerability. The rivers make Sanyuan the natural hub for grain shipment, timber rafting, and passenger travel across Sangguo, and the city's granaries feed armies and capitals alike. The sound of water is everywhere—the rush of the confluence, the creak of waterwheels, the splash of ferry poles—and the smell is of wet grain, fresh-cut timber, and the fish that thrive where two rivers mix. Sanyuan is prosperous but nervous, for whoever controls the rivers controls the city, and all three states know it.
