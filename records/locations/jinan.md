The northern capital of Jin, and the largest city in Gaia. Two hundred thousand people live within its walls. Wide boulevards, canal crossings, the warlord's palace on the central hill, and the Great Academy where scholars learn strategy and philosophy. Beneath the imperial veneer, non-human artisans navigate creeping human-only state restrictions; in the stonemason quarters, dwarven master [[character:shi-bao|Shi Bao]] of [[org:chu-dwarven-mechanics|The Clandestine Iron and Jade Guild]] carves load-bearing vaulted bridges under an assumed guild permit. The markets are enormous — silks from Chu, iron from Wei, spices from [[location:al-khayzar|Al-Khayzar]] via the Old Road.
