The northern capital of Jin, and the largest city in Gaia. Two hundred thousand people live within its walls. Wide boulevards, canal crossings, the warlord's palace on the central hill, and the Great Academy where scholars learn strategy and philosophy. The markets are enormous — silks from Chu, iron from Wei, spices from [[location:al-khayzar|Al-Khayzar]] via the Old Road. The truce is three years old, and the younger officers at the Academy speak openly about how it should end.
