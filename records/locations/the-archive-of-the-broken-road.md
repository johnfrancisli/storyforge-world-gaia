The Archive of the Broken Road is the great scholarly institution of Khalesh, a long stone building adjoining the Warden's Keep, founded centuries ago by the first Road-Warden to collect and study the fragments of the Old Road and the lost empire that built it. Within, the archive is a series of cool dry halls lined with shelves of copied tablets, drawn rubbings, and bound notebooks, and with the original fragments too valuable or too fragile to sell, arranged by find-site and by the scholars' best guess at their meaning. The loremasters of the archive — a small order of dedicated scholars, some local, some come from [[location:zaffar|Zaffar]] or beyond — work at long tables copying inscriptions, comparing fragments, and assembling the slow puzzle of the Old Road's history, its builders, its language, and its fall. The air is still and dusty and smells of old stone, ink, and the dry fibrous smell of the paper they make from the desert agave. The archive is open to any scholar who comes in good faith, and the loremasters will read an inscription for a digger or a traveller for a small fee, but the deeper records — the maps of un-excavated sites, the theories about the djinn and the Old Road — are kept for the order alone. The head loremaster, a patient grey-haired woman, is said to know more of the Old Road than any living soul.
