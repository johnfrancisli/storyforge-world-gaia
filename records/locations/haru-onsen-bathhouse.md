The hot spring bathhouse at the village's center, fed by the spring that gives [[location:haru-shrine-town|Haru's Shrine Town]] its reason to exist. A low building of cedar and stone, steam rising through the slats of the changing room, the mineral smell of the water sharp and clean. The bath is a single pool, natural rock lined with smooth stone, hot enough to turn the skin red in minutes. The villagers gather here at dusk — the shrine keeper, the store owner, the stable master, the farmers — and the talk that happens in the steam is the village's real government. A partitioned section is reserved for the fox spirits who maintain the message shop; they bathe separately and later, and their water is said to be warmer. Travelers are welcomed for a small fee and a respectful quiet. The water is said to ease old wounds, and it does.
