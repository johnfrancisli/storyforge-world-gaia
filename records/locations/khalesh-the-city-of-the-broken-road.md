Khalesh is the desert-edge city of [[location:al-khayzar|Al-Khayzar]], built where the great sand-sea gives way to a hard gravel plain at the foot of the western escarpment, and where the fragments of the Old Road — the lost highway of a forgotten empire that once linked Gaia's old nations — emerge from the sand in broken stone pylons, collapsed bridges, and paving worn smooth by a thousand years of feet that no one remembers. The city is older and stranger than the others, its walls of blackened stone not mud-brick but the dressed blocks of the old builders, re-used and re-laid by generations who have forgotten their original shape, and within the walls the streets follow no grid but the ghost of the old road, wandering past half-buried arches, ancient cisterns, and statues whose faces the wind has worn to smooth blanks. Scholars, relic-hunters, and the agents of binding-families come here to buy fragments of the Old Road — carved stones, inscribed tablets, and the strange dark-metal tokens that sometimes turn up in the sand and are believed to be djinn-work. The city lives on this trade and on the hard grazing of the desert-edge, and its people are quiet, watchful, and superstitious, dressed in dust-coloured wool against the wind, and given to hanging old road-fragments above their doors as wards. The air is dry and smells of dust, hot stone, and the faint ozone of the desert storms that sweep the escarpment.
