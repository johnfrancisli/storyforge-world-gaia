At the deepest accessible level of [[location:val-ironhold|Ironhold]], seven levels below the Great Gate, the Everflame Forge burns in a cavern of raw, unfinished stone where the heat is so intense that the air itself seems to bend and scream. The Everflame is a vent of subterranean fire, a river of molten rock that the dwarves have channeled into a controlled flow and used as a forge-fire for millennia—a fire so hot that it can work metals no surface forge can touch, including mithril-alloy and the legendary deepsteel that is [[location:val-ironhold|Ironhold]]'s most closely guarded secret. The forge is tended by the Forge-Priests, a brotherhood of warrior-smiths who treat the Everflame as a living deity, chanting hymns to the fire as they work, their voices lost in the roar of the flame and the ring of hammers on metal that glows white-hot. The air burns the lungs of any non-dwarf who enters without a wet cloth over the face, and even the dwarves work in shifts of no more than two hours. The forge produces the hold's greatest treasures—deepsteel weapons, mithril-alloy armor, and the enchanted runesteel that only the Forge-Priests know how to make—and its output is reserved for the Thane's household, the clan's champions, and the Crown's most urgent commissions.
