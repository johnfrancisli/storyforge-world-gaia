A small establishment near the shrine, run by a fox spirit who goes by the name Kiko and maintains the message service that links [[location:haru-shrine-town|Haru's Shrine Town]] to the next ward-point along the Cedar Road. The shop is a single room with a low desk, a charcoal brazier, and a cushion that is always warm, as if someone had just left it. Messages are written on thin paper, folded into fox-tags, and delivered along the ward-line by courier foxes faster than any horse — though only to shrines on the network, and only for those the foxes choose to serve. Kiko charges in copper or in favors, and her ledger of favors is longer than her ledger of coin. She is polite, sharp, and old in a way that does not show. The villagers treat her with careful respect. The shrine keeper treats her as a colleague.
