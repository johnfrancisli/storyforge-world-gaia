The shogun's palace on its hill, visible from every quarter of the capital — white plaster walls, dark grey tile, and a five-story main keep that catches the moonlight that gives the city its name. The outer wards are administrative halls where the city magistrate and the shogunate's clerks conduct the business of governance. The inner wards are the Ashikara household's private quarters, closed to all but family and the most senior retainers. The old shogun has not been seen in the inner gardens this season, and the senior retainers take his instructions through a screen. The palace wardstones are the strongest in the nation, maintained by the shrine network's master keeper, and they hum at a frequency that makes yokai uneasy within the walls. The throne room is kept ready. The moon-throne has not been sat in publicly since autumn.
