The Hanging Gate is the main entry to [[location:ver-altacanopy|Alta Canopy]], a long triple-braided rope bridge that climbs from a stone anchor on the forest floor up through the understory to the lowest inhabited platform. The ascent takes the better part of an hour for a laden climber, and the bridge sways and creaks with every step, winding around the buttress roots and through curtains of hanging moss and orchids. Wardens posted at the top inspect every visitor, checking for signs of blight-rot or spirit-possession before allowing them onto the city platforms — a green cord is tied around the wrist of those cleared, a red cord around those sent back down. The smell here is of wet bark, sweat, and the sharp resin the wardens smear on the hand-ropes to improve grip. Monkeys sometimes swarm the bridge to beg or steal, and the wardens keep slings and dried fruit to manage them. At dawn the gate is busiest, with hunters returning with game and traders arriving with clay and salt from below.
