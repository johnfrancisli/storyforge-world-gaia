The central gathering island of the Tide Archipelago. A ring of low islands around a calm lagoon, connected by canoe bridges and walkways. The great meeting house sits on the largest island, open-walled, facing the sea. This is where the wayfinder guilds meet and where inter-island disputes are settled. The shore has crept inward — the outer islets that used to hold fishing huts are underwater now. The lagoon is still beautiful, turquoise and calm, but the people here talk about where they will go when the water comes for the meeting house.
