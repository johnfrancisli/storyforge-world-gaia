The central gathering island of the Tide Archipelago. A ring of low islands around a calm lagoon, connected by canoe bridges and walkways, where agile cat-folk pearl divers of [[org:tide-pearl-guild|The Pearl-Blood Divers]] harvest black pearls from the outer reef drops. Foreign expertise has also arrived: the Mizuhito canal hydraulician [[character:feng-yulan|Feng Yulan]] works with island elders to design living coral floodgates against rising sea levels. Meanwhile, in the lantern-lit haven taverns near the anchorage, the alluring demon temptress [[character:malyssa-the-siren|Malyssa the Siren]] lures privateer captains into high-stakes dice games, winning their secret navigation charts.
