The Tide-Moot Hall is the seat of [[location:ver-tidalreach|Tidal Reach]]'s government, the largest building in the city, built on a massive raft-platform of lashed logs and floating barrels anchored at the harbour mouth so that it rises and falls with the tide and always moves gently underfoot. The hall is open-sided, its roof of woven palm-thatch held up by carved hardwood pillars painted with blue and white wave-patterns, and its floor is a deck of split bamboo that flexes with the swell. The Tide-Chancellor's high seat is a chair of driftwood and mother-of-pearl inlay at the far end, and the Moot's benches run in a broad horseshoe around a central speaking-pole carved as a leaping fish. When the Moot is in session the hall is crowded and noisy, captains and guild-elders shouting over the lap of water and the cries of gulls, and the Tide-Chancellor pounds the speaking-pole to restore order with a boom that echoes across the anchorage. A great conch horn hangs by the seat, blown only to summon the Moot in emergency or to mark a ruling. The air smells of salt, tar, and the palm-wine that is always served at moots.
