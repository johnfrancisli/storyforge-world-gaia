At the heart of [[location:hra-eyrby|Eyrby]], its ridge-pole carved with sheaves of barley and its doorposts painted the red of ripe rowan, Jarl Sigrid Barley-Mother's longhouse is the seat of the southern farmland's rule—a broad, low hall of turf and timber built more for warmth than for war, its walls thick with sod and its roof a deep thatch of barley straw that turns gold in the summer sun. The hall is smaller and lower than the High Jarl's, but cozier: a single fire-pit warms the whole room, the smoke curling up through a roof-hole that has blackened the high beams to a glossy obsidian, and the benches are padded with sheepskin and barley-straw cushions that smell of dust and malt. The jarl's high seat is a carved chair of apple-wood, and beside it stands the Harvest-Horn, a great drinking-horn bound in silver, from which Sigrid toasts the first sheaf of every harvest and the last sheaf of every fall. The walls are hung not with shields but with farming tools—decorated sickles, a prized plow-share, and the great iron cauldron used for the harvest feast—and the air is a permanent, gentle fog of baking bread, malt-smoke, and the sweet, sleepy smell of hay. Here Sigrid holds the Thing of [[location:hra-eyrby|Eyrby]], settles land-disputes, and hosts the harvest feasts that are the social highlight of the farming year.
