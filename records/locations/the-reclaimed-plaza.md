The Reclaimed Plaza is the great square before the pyramid, once the ceremonial heart of the old city and now the living heart of the new settlement. The flagstones have been cleared of jungle and swept clean, though a few trees and shrubs are deliberately left growing in the corners where the shamans say the forest-spirits dwell. In the mornings the plaza is a market — traders from [[location:iara|Iara]] and the river cities spread mats of salt, fish-hooks, steel, and cloth, while the settlers sell jade and quartz panned from the ruins, medicinal plants gathered from the overgrown temples, and the copied glyph-rolls that scholars prize. In the afternoons it becomes a work-yard where limestone blocks are shaped and hauled to restoration sites. In the evenings it is a gathering-place, where the whole community eats together around cookfires and the shamans tell the old stories carved on the pyramid walls by lamplight. Children play among the worn serpent-carvings, and a colony of oropendolas nests in the plaza trees, their hanging woven nests swaying overhead and their strange liquid calls echoing off the stone.
