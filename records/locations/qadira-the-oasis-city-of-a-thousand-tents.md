Qadira is the great caravan city of [[location:al-khayzar|Al-Khayzar]], risen around the largest oasis in the western desert — a circle of date palms and tamarisk seven bowshots across, whose heart is a long lake of cold blue water that never runs dry though the sand presses in on every side. The city is half permanent and half seasonal: a core of mud-brick towers, lime-washed in white and pale blue, where the oasis families live year-round, surrounded by an ever-shifting ring of tents and camel-pens where the caravan-clans gather between journeys, their striped pavilions and banner-poles crowding the sand for half a mile in every direction. The smell of Qadira is the smell of arrival — camel-dung smoke, roasting coffee, saddle-leather, and the sweet green breath of the date groves set against the dry mineral smell of the desert. At the water's edge women draw water in clay jars, children dive from the stone quays, and the dates are harvested by men swaying in the high fronds. The caravan-clans bargain here for water-rights, salt, dates, and the precious cargoes of [[location:zaffar|Zaffar]] that will travel the long roads to far nations. At night a thousand fires dot the tent-ring and the call to evening prayer rises from the oasis mosque over the lowing of camels.
