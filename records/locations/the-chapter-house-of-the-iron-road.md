A stone-and-timber hall on the town's eastern edge, strategically positioned to overlook both the King's Road and the Old Salt Route, the Chapter House of the Iron Road is the base of the Knights of the Iron Road—a small but active military order dedicated to keeping the kingdom's trade routes safe from bandits, monsters, and the opportunistic lawlessness that plagues any crossroads town. The chapter house is a fortified building of grey stone, its walls marked with the pockmarks of thrown stones and the occasional arrow, its gate reinforced with iron, and its courtyard always holding a few saddled horses ready for patrol. The knights—perhaps thirty in number—are a practical, unglamorous order, more concerned with road patrols and caravan escorts than with holy vows and glorious charges, and their cloaks of road-brown and iron-grey reflect their workmanlike ethos. The air in the chapter house smells of leather, horse, and the dust of the road, and the common room is a place of maps, duty rosters, and the quiet exhaustion of men who have ridden thirty miles and will ride thirty more tomorrow. The Chapter Master, a lean, sun-weathered man named Ser Garrick Roadborn, is a former caravan guard who earned his spurs through competence rather than birth, and his knights are similarly drawn from the practical end of the social spectrum. They are not the kingdom's most glamorous order, but the merchants of [[location:val-crossford|Crossford]] would be lost without them.
