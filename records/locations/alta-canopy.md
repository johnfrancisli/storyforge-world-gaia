Alta Canopy is [[location:verdania|Verdania]]'s great tree-city, suspended a hundred feet above the forest floor in the crowns of ancient Brazil-nut and kapok trees that have grown together over centuries into a living lattice. Rope bridges woven from liana and spider-silk crisscross between platforms carved from split bamboo and broad palm leaves, where agile cat-folk bridge runners like [[character:izel-amaru|Izel Amaru]] sprint between suspended huts. In the secluded upper terraces, sylvan forest elves of [[org:ver-canopy-circle|The Circle of the High Canopy]]—led by elder botanist [[character:lyra-moonleaf|Lyra Moonleaf]] and spirit-weaver [[character:sylva-orchid|Sylva Orchid]]—cultivate luminous spirit orchids and maintain their ancestral bond with the river shaman [[character:nara|Nara]]. Hornbills and scarlet macaws nest in the upper branches alongside the people, and the constant chatter of birds mingles with the laughter of children chasing each other across swaying bridges. Lamplight from clay oil-lamps turns the whole canopy amber at dusk, making it glow like a constellation trapped in the leaves.
