Alta Canopy is Verdania's great tree-city, suspended a hundred feet above the forest floor in the crowns of ancient Brazil-nut and kapok trees that have grown together over centuries into a living lattice. Rope bridges woven from liana and spider-silk crisscross between platforms carved from split bamboo and broad palm leaves, swaying gently in the wet wind that carries the thick green smell of sap, rotting fruit, and woodsmoke from a thousand cookfires. Hornbills and scarlet macaws nest in the upper branches alongside the people, and the constant chatter of birds mingles with the rhythmic thunk of axes shaping timber and the laughter of children chasing each other across swaying bridges. Lamplight from clay oil-lamps turns the whole canopy amber at dusk, making it glow like a constellation trapped in the leaves. The people here are long-limbed and sure-footed, dressed in feathered cloaks and dyed bark-cloth, and they speak of the forest floor below the way sailors speak of the deep sea — with respect, and a healthy fear of what moves in the dark.
