The Stair Market is the beating heart of [[location:ver-cascadeexchange|Cascade Exchange]], a long narrow bazaar that climbs the cliff-face in a series of stone steps, each step a row of stalls and workshops tucked under overhangs and behind canvas awnings. Traders hawk river-gold panned from the falls, dried fish, cacao in bitter blocks, western steel tools and copper ingots, dyed cloth from the jungle, jade and quartz amulets, fermented cacao wine, and the maps of the interior drawn on bark-paper that command high prices from foreign explorers. The steps are slick with spray and crowded with porters, muleteers, and rivermen shouting to be heard over the falls, and the press of bodies is so thick at midday that movement is measured in shuffles. Money-changers sit at small tables cut into the cliff wall, their scales clicking, and the Falls Factor's tax-collectors in their green sashes work the crowd noting every deal. The smell is of wet stone, roasted cacao, animal dung, and the sharp acid of dye-vats dripping from a terrace above.
