The beating heart of Kawazaki — a crescent of piers and moorings wide enough to hold two hundred vessels. Long pontoons of dark cedar bob in the swell, lashed together with rope the color of rust. Stevedores haul rice sacks and lacquer crates; a kappa works the rope coils on the eastern pier, tolerated because no one ties a knot faster. The harbor shrine sits at the end of the main pier, a small red torii above the tide line, where sailors drop copper coins before a voyage. The harbor master's tower rises at the harbor's throat, flying the Ashikara mon. Fog horns sound at dusk. The water is dark and cold and moves with things that are not always fish.
