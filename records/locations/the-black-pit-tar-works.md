At the inland edge of town, where the forest begins and the air is a permanent, eye-stinging fog of woodsmoke and pine-resin, the Black-Pit Tar-Works is [[location:hra-drekahofn|Drekahöfn]]'s tar-mill—the operation that produces the pitch and tar that waterproof every hull, every seam, and every rope in [[location:hrafnland|Hrafnland]]'s fleet. The works is a cluster of tar-pits—deep, stone-lined trenches roofed with turf and stacked with pine root-wood that is burned slow and airless, dripping a thick, black, acrid tar into collection-troughs at the pit's base—and the air for a quarter-mile downwind is heavy with the smell, which the tar-workers no longer notice and which every visitor never forgets. The tar-master, a black-stained, bandy-legged man named Grímmur Black-Pit, runs the works with a crew of a dozen who tend the slow fires day and night in shifts, judging the burn by the color of the smoke and the smell of the drip, and who are paid in silver and a share of every barrel shipped. The tar is barrelled, rolled to the shipyard, and applied hot to every plank-seam and rope-fiber before a ship puts out, and a shipwright will not launch a hull whose tar is not Grímmur's, for a seam that leaks is a seam that sinks, and the Black-Pit tar has never leaked in living memory. The works also produces the pine-pitch used for torches and the resin used for the carvers' gilding, and its fires have not been fully out in three generations.
