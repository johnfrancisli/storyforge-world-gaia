The spice capital of [[location:al-khayzar|Al-Khayzar]]. A city of domes and minarets, sun-baked sandstone walls, and narrow streets that open suddenly into courtyard markets. The Star Bazaar opens after sundown and runs until dawn — lanterns, silk canopies, the smell of cardamom and saffron. Here, foreign travelers meet: Hrafnish merchant [[character:einar-saltstride|Einar Saltstride]] trades northern walrus ivory, while rainforest herbalist [[character:iara-blossom|Iara Blossom]] studies desert wax preservation techniques. Behind gossamer curtains, the silk-veiled demon enchantress [[character:qamara-the-beguiling|Qamara the Beguiling]] outwits wealthy merchant princes like [[character:walid-al-tujjar|Walid al-Tujjar]] in high-stakes trade contracts.
