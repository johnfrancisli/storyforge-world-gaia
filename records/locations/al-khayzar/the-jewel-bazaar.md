The Jewel Bazaar is the market of Naqd, a single long street of vaulted shops cut into the cliff-face along the upper terrace, where the cut and polished stones of the mountain are sold to the caravans and the agents of the binding-families and the wealthy of [[location:zaffar|Zaffar]]. The shops are small and dark, lit by oil-lamps and by the fires of the lapidaries' wheels, and the stones are displayed on black velvet in locked cases — turquoise in blue-green clusters, lapis in deep blue blocks, carnelian in orange-red cabochons, and the rare green beryl that the binding-families prize for the binding of air-djinn, each stone held to the lamp and turned by the dealers who know every flaw and fire. The haggling is slow, quiet, and intense, for the sums are large and the stones easily swapped for glass, and the caravan-masters come with their own testers who file and weigh and hold each stone to the sun. Beyond the finished stones the bazaar sells rough ore, silver and copper ingots, mine-timber, lamp-oil, and the hard bread and dried meat that the mine-workers live on. The smell is of stone-dust, oil, hot metal from the lapidaries' wheels, and the smoke of the charcoal fires.
