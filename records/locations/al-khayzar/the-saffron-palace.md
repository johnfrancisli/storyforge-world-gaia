The Saffron Palace is the seat of the Sheikh of [[location:zaffar|Zaffar]] and the heart of [[location:al-khayzar|Al-Khayzar]]'s spice kingdom, a vast complex of lime-washed courtyards, tiled halls, and shaded gardens set on a low rise at the city's centre, its walls washed the colour of saffron-milk and its gates of bronze cast with the forms of lions and frankincense trees. Within, the halls are paved in cool marble veined with gold, their walls faced in tiles of lapis and turquoise set in arabesques that catch the light from high lattice windows and scatter it in coloured patterns across the floor. Fountains of white marble play in every courtyard, and the air is thick with the smell of rosewater, sandalwood incense, and the spice-warehouses that back onto the palace — saffron, cardamom, cinnamon, and the pepper that is the Sheikh's greatest treasure. The Sheikh's throne is a low seat of carved sandalwood draped with silk, set in the Hall of Audiences where petitioners, merchants, and binding-family elders come to be heard, and where the viziers and scribes of the spice-administration work at low desks piled with ledgers and tallies. Guards in white tunics and silver-crescent helms stand at every door, and eunuchs and slaves move silently through the inner courts. The palace is both residence and counting-house, and the clink of the spice-scales is audible even in the gardens.
