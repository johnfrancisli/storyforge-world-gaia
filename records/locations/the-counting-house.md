A two-story stone building near the crossroads, its facade painted with the Merchant Guild's blue-and-silver crest and its shutters reinforced with iron bars, the Counting House is the [[location:val-crossford|Crossford]] chapter of the Merchant Guild of [[location:val-rivenspire|Rivenspire]]—the administrative center from which the Guild-Steward governs the town's commerce and collects the tolls and pitch fees that fund its growth. The ground floor is a public counting room where merchants pay their dues, register their caravans, and purchase trade licenses, and the clerks who staff it are efficient, impersonal, and utterly incorruptible—or at least, incorruptible at the prices a [[location:val-crossford|Crossford]] merchant can afford. The upper floor is the Guild-Steward's office and residence, a comfortable but not lavish suite from which the Steward—a sharp-eyed, middle-aged man named Vorn Coldtally—manages the town's affairs with a ledger in one hand and a quill in the other. The Counting House smells of ink, fresh parchment, and the faint, ever-present scent of coin, and its strong-room, accessible through a trapdoor behind the clerk's desk, holds the town's toll revenue in locked iron boxes. The Counting House is the closest thing [[location:val-crossford|Crossford]] has to a seat of government, and Vorn Coldtally, though he holds no title nobler than Steward, is the most powerful man in town.
