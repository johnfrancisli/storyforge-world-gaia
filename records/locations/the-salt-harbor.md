The Salt Harbor is the great harbour of Basur, the sheltered water between the black volcanic breakwaters where the ships of [[location:al-khayzar|Al-Khayzar]] and a dozen foreign ports lie at anchor or tied to the long stone quays, a forest of masts and lateen yards so thick a child could cross the harbour by jumping deck to deck. The quays are a continuous roar of unloading — stevedores hauling bales and barrels, crane-teams swinging cargo from the holds, porters carrying spice and silk and salt in endless streams to the warehouses that back the waterfront, and the shouts of captains and harbour-officers cutting through the creak of rigging and the slap of water on stone. The ships are of every kind — the great spice-dhows of [[location:al-khayzar|Al-Khayzar]], the copper-hulled galleys of the western kingdoms, the slender pirate-cutters that now trade under Basuri papers, and the fishing-boats that put out at dawn and return at dusk heavy with the silver fish of the strait. The smell is of salt, tar, hemp, fish, and the spice that spills from broken bales onto the wet stone, and the gulls are a constant screaming cloud above the quays. At night the harbour is lit by fish-oil lamps on every post and the lights of the ships, and the waterfront taverns open their doors.
