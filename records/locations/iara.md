The river capital of [[location:verdania|Verdania]], built on stilts over a great river. Houses of woven wood and painted clay, connected by walkways and canoe bridges. The market floats — boats and rafts selling fish, fruit, feathers, and herbs. The river-folk are the majority here. The shamans' hall sits at the center of the city, the largest structure, where the elder shamans maintain the oldest spirit-pacts. The river is rising, and the lowest walkways are already underwater at high tide.
