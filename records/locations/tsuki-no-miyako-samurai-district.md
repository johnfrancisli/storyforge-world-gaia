The ordered grid of walled compounds on the slope between the palace and the river district, where the Ashikara retainers and the houses of the loyal clans maintain their city residences. The lanes are swept, the walls are whitewashed, and the gates carry the mon of the houses within. The district is quieter than the river district and colder than the tea quarter — this is where the shogunate's military and administrative class lives, and the atmosphere is one of controlled formality. Each compound has a training yard; the sound of practice is the district's dawn bell. The senior retainers' houses are nearest the palace, their compounds larger, their walls higher. The younger retainers and the lesser clan houses are further down the slope, where the lanes narrow and the formality eases, very slightly, into something almost like a neighborhood.
