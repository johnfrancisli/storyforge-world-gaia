The Weaver's Reach is the craft quarter of Iara, a cluster of stilt-house workshops along a quiet back-channel where the weavers, dyers, and feather-workers of the city have their workshops, chosen for the calm water that lets them set out their work on platforms and drying-racks without the current carrying it away. The sounds are of looms clacking, dyers stirring great clay vats of indigo, annatto, and brazilwood dye, and the soft murmur of women knotting feathers into cloaks and headdresses. Bark-cloth is beaten from inner bark on stone anvils with wooden mallets, then dyed and hung in long coloured sheets from the house-fronts to dry, turning the back-channel into a fluttering wall of red, yellow, black, and blue. The smell is of dye-vats — sharp, sour, vegetable — and of the mordant urine that fixes some colours, and of damp bark. The weavers' guild mistress, an old woman with hands stained permanently blue, oversees quality and settles disputes, and her word is law in the quarter. Fine work — feathered cloaks, painted bark-cloth, dyed cotton — is sold to the chief, the river-clans, and exported to Cascade Exchange and Tidal Reach.
