The orderly grid of walled compounds where the Kagami retainers live, on the slope between the castle and the forge quarter. Each compound is built around a small training yard; the sound of wooden swords on wood carries across the district from dawn. The retainers are loyal — not bought, not bullied, but bound by generations of service and the simple fact that the Kagami have never lost. The district has its own bathhouse, its own small shrine to the clan's founder, and a dojo where the senior retainers teach a sword style the Kagami do not allow to be taught anywhere else. The retainers' children play in the lanes with wooden swords before they can read. It is quiet here, and watchful.
