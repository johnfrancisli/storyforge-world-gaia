The Gilded Drake is the most famous tavern in [[location:crownhaven|Crownhaven]], a four-story timber-and-plaster house on the square's western edge that has been pouring ale since before the city had walls. Its common room is a cavern of dark wood, low beams, and a hearth big enough to roast an ox, around which travelers, sellswords, off-duty guardsmen, and the occasional disguised noble gather on benches worn smooth by generations of backsides. The air is a warm fog of pipe smoke, spilled ale, mutton grease, and the distinctly human smell of too many bodies in too little space. A bard in the corner competes with the roar of conversation, and the barkeep—a mountain of a woman named Hesta who remembers every face and every tab—presides over the tap with the authority of a general. Rooms upstairs range from a straw mattress in a shared loft to a private chamber with a lock and a window, and the quality of the clientele rises with the floor. Rumors are the Gilded Drake's true currency, and more plots, alliances, and betrayals have been hatched over its tables than in half the council chambers of Valdris.
