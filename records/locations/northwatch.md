Where the fertile plains of central Valdris give way to the frozen bogs and black pines of the Pale Reach, the garrison town of Northwatch huddles behind its double ring of palisade walls, a hard-edged outpost where the kingdom's northern frontier is held by stubbornness, steel, and badly needed reinforcements. The wind never stops here—it moans through the watch-towers and snaps the royal banner stiff as a board—and snow lies on the ground eight months of the year. Log-and-stone barracks cluster around a central keep of grey granite, their chimneys trailing smoke that smells of pine tar, mutton fat, and the sour tang of cheap ale. Soldiers in fur-lined cloaks patrol the walls with stamping boots and red ears, while trappers, hedge-witches, and the occasional frost-bearded northman come to trade furs and cured meat for iron and salt. The town has been burned and rebuilt three times, and the graveyard is larger than the market square. Every child learns to use a bow before they learn to read, and every door is barred by nightfall. Northwatch is not a place anyone visits for pleasure, but the Knights of the Pale Vow who garrison it are among the most decorated in the realm, and the Crown knows that without them the north would be lost within a season.
