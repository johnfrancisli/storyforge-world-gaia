The Cathedral of the Dawnfire is Crownhaven's spiritual heart, a soaring edifice of white granite and rose-tinted glass that catches the morning sun and sets the entire eastern quarter aglow in shades of amber and rose. Its twin spires, each crowned with a bronze flame that the priests light at every sunrise, can be seen from the city gates, and pilgrims come from across Valdris to touch the Dawnfire Stone—a relic said to have been carried from the heart of a dying star. Inside, the nave is vast and hushed, its floor a mosaic of the cosmos, its columns wrapped in carvings of saints and dragons. The smell of beeswax candles and burning incense hangs thick as a veil, and the chanting of the Dawnfire priesthood rises and falls in a rhythm that seems to slow time itself. Knights who have sworn holy vows kneel in the side chapels, their armor gleaming in the colored light, and the High Priestess herself conducts the Dawnfire Mass each morning to a congregation that spills out into the square. The cathedral also serves as the site of royal coronations, weddings, and funerals, binding the Crown to the Dawnfire faith in a pact older than the kingdom itself.
