The commercial heart of Jin-an stretches from the eastern gate along the main road toward the river, a teeming avenue of permanent shops, canvas stalls, and street vendors selling everything a great capital demands. Silks from Chu, iron tools from Wei, grain from [[location:san-river-junction|Sanyuan]], and foreign luxuries from [[location:san-silkroad-city|Yunmeng]] all pass through this market, alongside local craftsmen—paper-makers, lantern-builders, comb-cutters, and noodle vendors whose stalls fill the air with the smell of boiling broth and scallion oil. The market is divided into guild-regulated sections, each marked by a banner, and prices are posted on wooden boards that are updated at dawn. City guards patrol the avenue in pairs, and the market bell rings to mark opening and closing, after which trade is forbidden and shutters come down.
