Tidal Reach is Verdania's river-mouth city, built where the Verde River widens into a great estuary before pouring into the grey-green sea. The city stands on stilts and rafts over the tidal flats and shallows, its houses raised on hardwood pilings driven into the mud, connected by plank walkways and rope bridges that rise and fall with the tide and creak and groan as the water shifts beneath them. At high tide the sea laps at the floors and fish swim under the houses; at low tide the mudflats are exposed and the people wade out to dig for clams, crabs, and the odd manatee, the air thick with the briny smell of salt mud, rotting seaweed, and drying fish. The harbour mouth is guarded by a pair of ancient stone pillars — all that remains of an older port — and the fishing fleet of outrigger canoes and larger sailing dhows rocks at anchor, their hulls crusted with barnacles and painted in blue and white stripes. The people here are river-folk and sea-folk mixed, darker-skinned than the jungle Verdani, dressed in loose salt-bleached cloth and palm hats, and they speak with a drawl that marks them even in Iara. The city smells of salt, smoke, and the sweet heavy scent of palm-wine fermenting in the sun.
