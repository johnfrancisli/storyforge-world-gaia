The river capital of [[location:verdania|Verdania]], built on stilts over a great river. Houses of woven wood and painted clay, connected by walkways and canoe bridges. The market floats — boats and rafts selling fish, fruit, feathers, and herbs. Agile cat-folk warriors like the chief's guard [[character:sangue|Sangue]] patrol the walkways, while visiting island wayfinder [[character:keahi-oceanstrider|Keahi Oceanstrider]] pilots her outrigger through the delta to map freshwater tidal surges. Out in the treacherous mangrove outskirts, the feral demon siren [[character:morrigan-the-wild|Morrigan the Wild]] haunts the marsh mist, beguiling timber bosses into bottomless bogs to protect the mother grove from foreign axes.
