The Mist Shrine is the spirit-lodge of [[location:ver-cascadeexchange|Cascade Exchange]], set on a ledge behind the curtain of the tallest fall, reached by a slick narrow path cut into the cliff behind the water. The constant spray keeps everything wet and glistening, and the shrine itself is a small structure of water-smoothed stones and green wood, half-hidden by ferns, with a low altar where the river-shaman makes offerings to the spirit of the falls — a being the locals call the Roaring Mother, said to be the soul of the river made visible in the white water. Offerings of flowers, cacao, carved fish-bone tokens, and sometimes a living bird released through the curtain are left on the altar, and the shaman reads the fall's mood in the patterns of the spray. The roar of the water is so loud here that ritual is conducted in gesture and drum-vibration rather than speech, the shaman beating a water-drum whose sound is said to carry to the river-spirits downstream. The air is cold and smells of wet stone and crushed green leaves. It is a place of power, and traders come here to make vows before dangerous journeys upriver.
