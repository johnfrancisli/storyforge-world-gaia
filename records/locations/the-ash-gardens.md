The Ash Gardens are the fertile terraces that stripe the lower southern slope of Motu Peleki where the volcanic ash settles deepest, turning the soil black and rich and growing the finest breadfruit, taro, and bananas in the archipelago. The gardens are laid in stone-walled terraces that step down the slope, irrigated by channels cut from a spring-fed stream, and each terrace is tended by a household or clan that holds hereditary rights to its produce. The breadfruit trees here grow twice the size of those on other islands, their broad leaves dark green and glossy with the mineral richness, and the taro corms are enormous, dug from the dark soil with wooden spades and roasted in earth-ovens — pits lined with hot stones and banana leaves that steam and smoke all day, filling the lower slope with the warm sweet smell of cooking starch and green leaves. The gardens are protected by the Fire-Voices' decree: any eruption that threatens them triggers an evacuation of the harvesters but the gardens themselves are never abandoned, replanted the moment the ash settles. Chickens scratch among the terraces, and children chase them with sticks, their laughter carrying up the slope.
