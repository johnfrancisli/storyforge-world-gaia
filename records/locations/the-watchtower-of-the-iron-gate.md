The Watchtower of the Iron Gate is the headquarters of the Crownhaven City Watch, a squat, iron-banded fortress of dark stone set into the city's main gatehouse, from which the Watch's captain commands a force of three hundred armsmen responsible for keeping order in the capital. The tower bristles with alarm bells, signal fires, and a gryphon-weathered mast from which the Watch's black-and-gold pennant flies day and night. Inside, the ground floor is a mustering hall where shifts are briefed over maps of the city's districts, its walls hung with wanted posters and chalkboards tracking crime patterns. The air smells of leather, lamp oil, and the sweat of men who have walked a long patrol. Cells in the basement hold the night's catch—drunkards, pickpockets, and the occasional murderer—awaiting the morning magistrate. The Watch is widely considered underfunded and overstretched, but its captain, a scarred veteran named Doran Black, is feared by the criminal element and respected by the Crown, and under his command the capital's streets are safer than those of any other city in the kingdom.
