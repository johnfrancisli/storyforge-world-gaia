The Riven Bridge is the spine of Rivenspire—a massive stone arch spanning the Greyfen Gorge, wide enough for two wagons to pass abreast, lined on both sides with statues of the river-gods and the merchant-lords who funded its construction. It is the only crossing of the gorge for a hundred miles in either direction, and every coin, crate, and traveler that moves between the north and south banks of the river must pass over it, which is why the bridge is also the most profitable piece of infrastructure in the kingdom. A toll-house at the bridge's center, a squat stone tower flying the Merchant Guild's banner, collects a copper per foot-traveler and a silver per wagon, and the revenue funds half the city's budget. The bridge is always crowded—merchants, pilgrims, soldiers, beggars, and the occasional noble carriage all jostle for space on the narrow roadway—and the air is thick with the roar of the gorge below, the shouts of the toll-collectors, and the smell of river spray and horse sweat. Pickpockets work the crowd with practiced ease, and the bridge's low parapet has been the last stop for more than one desperate debtor. At night, oil-lamps are lit along the parapet, and the bridge becomes a river of fire reflected in the black water below.
