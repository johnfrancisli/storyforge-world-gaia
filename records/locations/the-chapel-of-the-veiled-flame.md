Carved into a quiet ledge of the fortress's inner wall, the Chapel of the Veiled Flame is Dragonrest's place of worship—a small, solemn space of grey stone and dim light where the garrison's soldiers come to pray before battle and to honor their dead. A single altar of polished obsidian holds the Veiled Flame, a fire that burns without fuel or smoke behind a screen of thin alabaster, casting a soft, steady light that is said to soothe troubled hearts and grant courage to the faithful. The walls are carved with the names of every soldier who has died in the fortress's defense, a list that grows longer with every generation, and the air smells of cold stone, burnt offerings, and the faint sweetness of dried flowers laid at the altar by widows and orphans. A single chaplain, an aging knight who took holy orders after losing his sword-arm, tends the flame and offers counsel, and his voice is the only one that ever breaks the chapel's heavy silence. The Veiled Flame is the only light in Dragonrest that never goes out, and the garrison believes that if it ever does, the fortress will fall within the year.
