The Elder's Council Rock is the meeting-place of [[location:tide-makapaa|Makapaa]], a broad flat-topped boulder of grey basalt perched on the highest ledge of the village where the wind is strongest and the view reaches to the horizon on every side, its top worn smooth by generations of seated elders and ringed by low stone seats cut into the rock. The Cliff-Elders gather here at every new and full moon to hear disputes, allot fishing-grounds, and decide the island's affairs, their voices raised against the wind and the gull-cries, and the Wave-Speaker sits at the rock's center on a seat of driftwood draped with a net-cloth dyed red with sea-snail ink, the only colour on the grey rock. A standing stone at the rock's edge is carved with the tally-marks of every catch and every drowning as far back as memory, and the elders lay their hands on it before speaking truth. Petitioners wait on a lower ledge, called up by a conch-blast, and the whole village may gather on the surrounding ledges to listen. The air is clean, cold, and salt here at the island's crown, and on clear days you can see the dim blue shapes of the other islands on the horizon — [[location:lani-atoll|Lani Atoll]]'s palms, [[location:tide-motu-peleki|Motu Peleki]]'s smoke — and the elders gauge the weather and the fishing by the look of the sea from this height.
