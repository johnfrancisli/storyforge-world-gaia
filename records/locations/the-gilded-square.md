The great market square of Crownhaven, known to all as the Gilded Square, is a vast paved plaza at the city's heart where the kingdom's wealth is on daily, noisy, fragrant display. Stalls and pavilions crowd so thickly that the ground disappears beneath canvas, and the alleys between them are barely wide enough for two men to pass shoulder to shoulder. Here a dwarf from Ironhold haggles over a bolt of elven silk beside a Rivenspire factor inspecting a crate of southern spices, while a street conjurer draws a crowd with cantrips and a pie-seller shouts her wares over the din. The air is a layered tapestry of roasting meat, incense, horse sweat, and the sharp green scent of fresh-cut herbs. A bronze fountain at the square's center, shaped like a rearing dragon, spouts clear water that the thirsty and the destitute drink from alike. The City Watch maintains a post at the square's north corner, but the pickpockets and cutpurses treat the crowd as their personal treasury, and a traveler who does not watch their purse will soon be lighter by it.
