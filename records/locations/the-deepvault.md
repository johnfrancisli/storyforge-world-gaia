Behind a door of solid iron three feet thick, sealed with seven dwarven locks whose keys are held by seven different guild-masters and can only be opened when all seven are present simultaneously, the Deepvault is [[location:val-ironhold|Ironhold]]'s treasury and archive—the repository of the clan's wealth, its most precious relics, and the records of its six-thousand-year lineage. The vault is a cold, dry chamber of stone shelves and iron strong-boxes, its air still and mineral and untouched by the forge-heat of the levels above. Gold and silver ingots are stacked in neat rows, but the true treasures are the relic-chambers: a sealed case holding the Stonebook of the First Thane, a set of runesteel armor said to have been worn by the clan's founder, a phial of the first metal ever pulled from the mountain's heart, and the Lineage Stone—a monolith inscribed with the name of every dwarven child born to the clan, stretching back to the founding. Two Stoneguard, dwarven warriors sworn to defend the vault with their lives, stand at eternal attention beside the door, and the vault's location is known only to the Thane, the seven guild-masters, and the Stoneguard themselves. A thief who breached the Deepvault would steal not just wealth but the history of a people, and for that reason the clan would pursue such a thief to the ends of Gaia.
