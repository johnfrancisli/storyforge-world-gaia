In the sheltered square between the inner palisade and the keep, the Frost Market is [[location:val-northwatch|Northwatch]]'s commercial heart—a muddy, crowded square of wooden stalls and canvas lean-tos where trappers, northmen, and garrison quartermasters trade the goods that keep the town alive through the long winters. Furs are the primary currency—wolf, bear, ermine, and the thick white pelts of the snow-fox that fetch a high price in the southern cities—and they hang from every stall in a patchwork of browns, greys, and whites that gives the market the look of a living beast. The smell of cured hides, smoked fish, pine smoke, and cheap ale is overpowering, and the cold turns every breath to fog. A northman in shaggy furs haggles with the garrison's quartermaster over a side of smoked reindeer, while a hedge-witch from the bogs sells frostwort poultices to soldiers whose ears and fingers have gone black with frostbite. There is no luxury here—no silk, no spice, no gold—but there is survival, and a good trade can mean the difference between seeing the spring and not. The market closes at midday, for no one in [[location:val-northwatch|Northwatch]] stays out after dark who can help it, and by the time the signal fires are lit the square is empty and the mud is freezing hard.
