The narrow lanes behind the harbor where the tea houses, sake dens, and river-girl establishments press shoulder to shoulder. Lantern light on wet stone, the sound of shamisen and a low drum, the smell of plum wine and pipe smoke and something sweeter from the upper rooms. This is where merchant deals are closed that the harbor master will never hear about, where samurai come off the ships and spend a season's pay in a night, and where a yokai in a painted face will pour your drink and ask you questions you will answer before you realize you have. The quarter is run by the Tea House Guild, and the guild is run by a woman no one has ever seen clearly. It is the loudest, warmest, most dangerous part of Kawazaki.
