The head shrine of the Ashikara ward network, set on a rise between the palace and the river district. A complex of cedar halls and moss gardens centered on a main sanctuary where the network's master keeper conducts the rituals that bind the nation's wardstones to their anchors. The shrine is the largest in Tsukuyomi — six priests, twelve acolytes, and a library of ward-craft that is smaller than the Shirogane archive but more current. The master keeper, a stern woman named Karin, is one of the few people the old shogun still sees regularly, and no one knows what is said in those meetings. The shrine's central wardstone is the network's root anchor; if it fails, every wardstone in the nation feels it. It has not failed. The shrine keeps a fox spirit in the inner garden, bound by an old pact, who carries messages between the network's shrines faster than any horse.
