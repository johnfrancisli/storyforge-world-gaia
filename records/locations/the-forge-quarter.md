Cut into a ravine on Weidu's northern face, the Forge Quarter is a cascade of smithies built into the rock itself, their chimneys channeled through natural fissures so the entire district breathes smoke and fire. The hammer-strike of metal on anvil is the district's unending rhythm, and the orange glow of furnaces turns the ravine walls molten at night. Master smiths forge the laminated steel swords Wei soldiers are famous for, as well as arrowheads, armor plates, and the iron grates that seal the city gates. Apprentices stoke the fires with coal hauled up from the lower mines, their faces permanently streaked with soot. The forge-masters hold secrets of steel-fold and edge-tempering passed only from father to son, and Wei steel is prized across Sangguo.
