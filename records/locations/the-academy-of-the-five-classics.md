The foremost center of learning in Sangguo, the Academy of the Five Classics sits in a walled grove of ginkgo trees on the northern edge of Jin-an, its lecture halls and dormitories arranged around a central library tower that holds the largest collection of scrolls in the known world. Scholars in grey robes debate philosophy, mathematics, military strategy, medicine, and astronomy beneath the ginkgoes, their voices rising in the structured cadence of formal argument. The smell is of old paper, ink, and the faint bitterness of ginkgo seeds crushed underfoot. Students from noble and common families alike study here, competing in examinations that determine placement in the Jin bureaucracy. The Academy's headmaster is appointed by the warlord but traditionally maintains a stance of intellectual independence that even the throne hesitates to challenge.
