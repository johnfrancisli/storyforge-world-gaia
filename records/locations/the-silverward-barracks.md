In a cleared grove at [[location:val-elfshade|Elfshade]]'s northern edge, where the Thornwhisper Forest thins toward the human lands, the Silverward Barracks house the city's small but formidable military force—the Silverwardens, an order of elven warrior-scouts who patrol the forest's borders and guard the approaches to the city. The barracks are a cluster of elegant but functional tree-platforms connected by rope bridges, each fitted with a rainshield of woven bark and a weapon rack of slender elven bows, silver-leafed spears, and leaf-bladed longswords. The Silverwardens are fewer than two hundred, but each is a century-trained fighter, and their captain, a scarred elf named Thandiel Silverbow who lost an eye to a dragon a century ago, is considered one of the finest archers alive. The air here smells of pine pitch, oiled bowstring, and the sharp, clean scent of whetted steel. Training is constant and silent—elves sparring in the canopy with a fluidity that makes human swordplay look like carpentry—and the wardens maintain a network of hidden lookouts throughout the forest that can relay a signal to the barracks in minutes. The Silverwardens answer to the Thornwhisper Court, but their real loyalty is to the forest itself.
