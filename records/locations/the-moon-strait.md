The open water between Tsukuyomi's western shores and [[location:verdania|Verdania]]'s eastern coast. The crossing takes a week in good weather, two in bad. Trade ships carry [[location:verdania|Verdania]]'s featherwork, river-pearls, and medicinal bark west; Tsukuyomi's lacquerware, silver, and shrine-craft east. Pirates operate from uninhabited volcanic islets along the route, striking at becalmed ships and vanishing into the fog that the strait is named for. On clear nights the moon lays a road of silver on the water so bright you could read by it.
