The largest building in [[location:san-longpass-town|Longpass Town]], the Broken Sword is a sprawling timber-and-stone tavern that serves as drinking hall, inn, black market, and unofficial embassy all at once. The main room is a cavern of rough-hewn tables lit by tallow candles, where mercenaries in battered armor drink rice wine by the jug and haggle over contracts with brokers who take a cut of every deal. The walls are hung with broken blades—trophy weapons from soldiers who died in the pass—and the air is thick with pipe smoke, the smell of mutton stew, and the tension of men who might be friends or enemies depending on the hour's pay. A back room, accessible only by a password that changes weekly, serves as a meeting place for spies and diplomats from all three states.
