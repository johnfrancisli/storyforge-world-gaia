The granite knob at the town's center, crowned by a squat three-story keep of dark stone and iron-banded timber. The walls are thick enough that the winter wind does not reach the inner halls. The governor's quarters are on the top floor; the garrison command room is on the second, where a map of the northern passes covers an entire wall, marked with pins for yokai sightings, patrol routes, and the names of posts where soldiers have died. The ground floor is an armory and a wardstone-charging chamber, where the gate wardstones are renewed on a rotation that no one dares miss. The keep has a well cut into the granite that has never run dry. The roof mounts a signal fire that, lit, can be seen by the next post down the Cedar Road within the hour.
