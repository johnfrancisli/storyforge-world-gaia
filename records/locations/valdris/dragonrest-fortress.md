The mountain fortress of the dragon-bonded knightly order, built into the peaks above [[location:crownhaven|Crownhaven]]. Stone walls scarred by dragonfire, a courtyard wide enough for a dragon to land, and a hall where the bonded knights once gathered. The hall is half-empty now. High above the fortress walls, the mystery of the Silence of the Drakes echoes through the empty caverns; only solitary female dragon-kin like [[character:valkyria-ashscale|Valkyria Ashscale]] keep watch over the abandoned wyrm-carved aeries. Below in the rocky mountain pass, wandering warriors such as the ronin caravan escort [[character:kenji-the-wanderer|Kenji the Wanderer]] stand guard over supply wagons crossing the treacherous heights.
