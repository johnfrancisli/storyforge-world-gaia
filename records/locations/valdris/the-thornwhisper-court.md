In the heart of [[location:val-elfshade|Elfshade]], where the greatest of the Thornwhisper oaks spreads its branches over a natural amphitheater of mossy roots and hanging lanterns, the Thornwhisper Court convenes beneath the open sky. There is no throne, no dais, no stone—only a circle of ancient roots worn smooth by millennia of elven elders who have sat upon them in council, and a canopy of leaves so dense that rain rarely penetrates. The Court meets at dusk, when the starlight lanterns kinden and the air fills with the scent of night-blooming jasmine and the low, melodic cadence of the Elvish tongue. Lord-Councilor Ithilandel, ancient even by elven reckoning, sits at the circle's head, his silver hair falling past his waist, his eyes the color of a winter sky. Petitioners—elven, human, and occasionally other—stand at the circle's edge and speak their case, and the elders debate in a language so beautiful that even those who cannot understand it find themselves moved to tears. Justice here is slow, deliberate, and absolute, and a ruling of the Thornwhisper Court has never been overturned by any power in Valdris, including the Crown.
