The Greyfen Quays are the pulsing, stinking, cacophonous lifeblood of [[location:val-rivenspire|Rivenspire]]—a line of stone piers and timber wharves stretching along both banks of the river below the gorge, where barges, ferries, and the occasional seagoing cog jostle for berthing space and the air is a thick cocktail of river mud, tar, fish, and the sweat of stevedores. Longshoremen haul crates of grain, ore, cloth, and wine along the quays from dawn to dusk, their backs bent and their hands raw, while bargemen curse the current and the customs officers in the same breath. Cranes of iron and timber swing loads between dock and deck with groaning precision, and the river is so thick with traffic that a barge can wait half a day for a berth during the trade season. The smell is overpowering to newcomers but invisible to the dockhands who have lived in it their whole lives. Beneath the quays, in the damp and the dark, smugglers move untaxed goods through tunnels that connect the river to the cellars of the waterside warehouses, and the City Watch's harbor patrol—six boats and a dozen men—barely scratches the surface of the trade that flows beneath the official ledger. Every legal cargo in [[location:val-rivenspire|Rivenspire]] starts or ends here, and a good portion of the illegal cargo does too.
