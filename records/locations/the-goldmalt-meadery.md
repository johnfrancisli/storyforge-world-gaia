On the sunny slope above the town, where the birch windbreak gives way to long, ordered rows of beehives and barley terraces, the Goldmalt Meadery is [[location:hra-eyrby|Eyrby]]'s pride and [[location:hrafnland|Hrafnland]]'s greatest mead-works—a cluster of low, turf-roofed malthouses and a single, larger brewing-hall whose chimneys trail a permanent sweet-smoke that the whole town knows as the smell of a good year. The meadery is run by the Mead-Guild, a family-craft order that has tended the same yeast-strain for nine generations and guards its malt-house secrets as fiercely as any rune-carver guards his marks. Inside the brewing-hall, great copper vats bubble over charcoal fires, and the air is a thick, humid fog of fermenting malt, honey, and the sharp, bright scent of brewer's herbs—juniper, bog-myrtle, and the wild thyme that grows only on the fjord's upper slopes. The master brewer, a white-haired, flour-dusted woman named Inga Goldmalt, can judge a mead's readiness by the sound of its ferment and has never released a bad barrel. The meadery's Goldmalt Mead is shipped to [[location:skaldvik|Skaldvik]] and from there to every hall in [[location:hrafnland|Hrafnland]], and a jarl who cannot serve Goldmalt at his feast is a jarl whose guests will talk.
