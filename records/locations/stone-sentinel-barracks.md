The largest single structure in [[location:san-weidu|Weidu]], the Stone Sentinel Barracks house the standing army in a series of tiered stone blocks stepped into the mountainside like a fortress within a fortress. Each block houses a company, and the courtyards between them are training grounds where soldiers drill in formation regardless of weather—snow, wind, or the biting cold of mountain nights. The smell is of horse dung, oiled leather, and stone dust, and the sound is the cadenced shout of drill instructors and the crack of wooden practice weapons. Every soldier rotates through wall duty, forge labor, and field training in cycles dictated by the military council. The barracks also hold the armory reserves—enough weapons and rations to sustain a siege for a year.
