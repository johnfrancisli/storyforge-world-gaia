The Shrine of the Mountain Heart is the spirit-shrine of Naqd, set deep in the oldest mine-gallery, a natural cavern of crystals and pools that the miners found long ago and have left uncut, believing it to be the heart of the mountain-djinn whose body is the stone and whose favour gives or withholds the veins. The cavern is vast and quiet, its walls encrusted with clusters of quartz and amethyst that catch the lamplight in a thousand points of colour, and at its centre a pool of still black water reflects the crystals and the faces of those who come to pray. The mine-shaman, an old ex-miner marked by the dust and the dark, tends the shrine, pouring offerings of oil and stone-dust into the pool and reading the mountain's mood in the way the lamplight moves on the crystals. The miners come here before a new gallery is cut, asking the mountain-djinn's leave, and after a cave-in, seeking its mercy, and the Gem-Houses, for all their greed, will not cut a stone from the cavern or allow a gallery to be driven toward it, fearing the mountain's anger. The air is still and cool and smells of wet stone and oil, and the only sound is the drip of water and the low chant of the shaman.
