The Trade-Master's Platform is the seat of governance in [[location:tide-vanua-tafola|Vanua Tafola]], a fortified raft-house of dark hardwood built on the most stable platform in the city, a double-deck of balsa and cork-wood anchored deep with stone weights so that it barely moves with the swell, its walls hung with the painted shields of the trading houses and the signal-flags of every island that trades here. Inside, the floors are polished bamboo worn smooth by generations of boots, and the Trade-Master's desk sits at the room's end beneath a great chart of the archipelago painted on bark-cloth, its islands marked with their trade-goods in coloured ink. Ledgers, duty-tallies, and sealed bark-paper letters from [[location:lani-atoll|Lani Atoll]] pile on the desk, and a clerk is always present to record rulings. The float-guard, a dozen men and women in blue sashes, are quartered on the lower deck, and a signal-tower on the roof flies the Trade-Master's flag and signals incoming traders with coloured pendants. The air smells of damp bamboo, ink, lamp-oil, and the strong palm-wine the Trade-Master drinks hot with pepper-root. From the platform's gallery you can see the whole lagoon — the market-float, the cordage-works, the guest-floats, the two sheltering islets — and the open sea beyond, where a lookout watches for foreign sails and storms.
