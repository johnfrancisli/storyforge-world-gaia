Motu Peleki is the fire-mountain island of the Tide Archipelago, a steep volcanic cone of black basalt and ropy lava that rises smoking from the warm sea, where cat-folk spring divers like [[character:leilani-vao|Leilani Vao]] plunge into warm volcanic lava tubes. Atop the sheer sea needles and basalt spires ringing the island, solitary sapphire dragon-kin like [[character:kaimana-deepscale|Kaimana Deepscale]] guard ancient sunken wyrm-shrines in the aftermath of the Silence of the Drakes. By night the crater casts a dull orange glow against the clouds and columns of white steam explode where lava-fingers reach the water, filling the air with hot stone, roasted breadfruit, and sea spray.
