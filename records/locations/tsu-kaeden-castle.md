The black castle on the ridge. Iron roof tiles that turn the rain the color of old blood, timber walls braced with bands of Kagami steel, and a central keep six stories high that can see the river road for miles in every direction. The inner bailey houses the Kagami household and two hundred retainers; the outer bailey holds the armory, the stables, and the guest hall where visiting samurai are received — and counted. The castle has never fallen. The walls are warded, though the Kagami wards are older and stranger than the Ashikara network, cut with symbols the shrine network does not use. Daimyo Kagami Renji holds court in the upper keep. He is fifty, calm, and rumored to be the best swordsman in the west. No one has tested the rumor.
