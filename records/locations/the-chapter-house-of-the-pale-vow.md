Within the inner palisade, occupying a long, low hall of stone and timber that is perpetually cold and perpetually busy, the Chapter House of the Pale Vow is the home of the Knights of the Pale Vow—the military order that garrisons Northwatch and holds the northern frontier against the things that come down from the frozen dark. The hall is spartan: a single long room with a hearth at one end, weapon racks along the walls, and straw pallets for the knights who are not on watch. The knights themselves are a hard-bitten, weather-beaten breed—men and women who have taken a holy vow to hold the Pale Reach until death or thaw, and whose order's badge, a white flame on a field of black, is worn on cloaks that are more often red with blood than white with snow. The air smells of steel oil, wet wool, and the faint, antiseptic tang of frostwort poultices from the infirmary alcove where the order's healer tends the wounded. The Chapter Master, a grey-haired, one-eyed woman named Ser Aldreth Frostfire, commands the order with a quiet authority that her knights would follow into a dragon's maw—which several of them have. The Pale Vow is the smallest of the kingdom's knightly orders but the most decorated, and its casualties are the highest, and every knight in the chapter house knows that their posting is a honor and a death sentence in equal measure.
