The Chief's Float is the seat of [[location:iara|Iara]]'s governance, a vast raft-platform of lashed balsa logs and cork-wood anchored at the heart of the river capital where the current is slowest and the water deepest. Upon it stands the chief's hall, a long low building of dark hardwood with walls of woven palm and a roof of layered thatch weighted with river-stones, its eaves carved with the forms of fish, herons, and anacondas and painted in black, red, and gold. Inside, a central hearth of sand and river-clay holds a fire fed with driftwood and resinous wood brought down from the canopy, and the smoke rises through a roof-gap, sweet and heavy. The chief's seat is a raised chair of polished ironwood draped with jaguar pelts, flanked by the carved speaking-staves of the river-clans. Petitioners wait on the landing-ramp and are called in by a herald who blows a conch. The walls are hung with tribute — featherwork, jade, river-gold, and the painted skulls of past chiefs — and the air smells of woodsmoke, wet wood, and the river itself, which laps audibly beneath the floor. Guards in bone-corselets stand at the doors.
