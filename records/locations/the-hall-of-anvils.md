The Hall of Anvils is the ceremonial and social heart of Ironhold—a vast underground chamber at the hold's third level, its ceiling a soaring vault of polished granite supported by twelve pillars carved into the likenesses of the twelve founding dwarven heroes. Great iron anvils, each the size of a wagon and polished to a mirror sheen by centuries of use, are set in a circle at the hall's center, and it is here that the Thane holds council, hears petitions, and presides over the great feasts that are the social cornerstone of dwarven life. The hall is lit by basins of eternal forge-fire that cast a warm, coppery light over everything, and the air is thick with the smell of roasting meat, black stout, and the mineral tang of deep stone. Tapestries of iron chain and bronze thread depict the clan's history, and the walls are hung with the arms and armor of heroes past—weapons that are still sharp and armor that is still sound, for dwarven steel does not rust. At feasts, the hall fills with hundreds of dwarves in their finest, the noise of eating, drinking, and boasting rising to a roar that shakes the stalactites, and the Thane's mead-horn, a relic carved from a single amethyst, is passed from hand to hand until every warrior has drunk to the clan's prosperity.
