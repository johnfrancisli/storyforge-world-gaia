On the hill overlooking [[location:san-silkroad-city|Yunmeng]]'s center, the Temple Quarter is a cluster of shrines and temples representing every faith whose traders have passed through the city—ancestor halls beside foreign fire-temples, a lotus shrine next to a desert god's sanctuary, and a great central altar where all faiths are welcome to offer together. The scent of a dozen different incenses mingles in the hilltop air, creating a perfume that belongs to no single tradition. Monks and priests of various orders walk the terraced paths in their distinct robes, and pilgrims from distant lands kneel beside local worshippers. The quarter is also [[location:san-silkroad-city|Yunmeng]]'s center of learning; scholars from different cultures exchange knowledge in a shared library at the hilltop, translating texts and mapping the stars in a spirit of curiosity that the warring states' capitals would never permit.
