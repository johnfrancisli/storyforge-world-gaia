South of the crossroads, a broad, open field of trampled grass and packed earth that becomes a sea of mud in the rain and a dust bowl in the dry season, the Fairground is where [[location:val-crossford|Crossford]]'s seasonal fairs are held—the great trade gatherings that draw merchants from across Valdris and beyond, and that transform the town from a busy crossroads into a city of canvas and commerce for two weeks at a stretch. During the fairs, the field is packed with pavilions, tents, and temporary stalls in a sprawling, unplanned grid, and the noise, the crowds, and the smell of a thousand competing wares—spices, wool, leather, livestock, potions, and the occasional exotic beast—create a sensory overload that overwhelms first-time visitors and energizes veteran traders. Between fairs, the Fairground is used for livestock grazing, militia drills, and the occasional public execution, and its only permanent structure is a raised wooden platform at the center—the Guild-Steward's stand, from which fair rules are proclaimed and disputes are adjudicated on the spot. The ground is littered with the detritus of past fairs—broken crate boards, stray coins, the occasional lost boot—and a determined scavenger can make a modest living picking through the field the morning after a fair closes.
