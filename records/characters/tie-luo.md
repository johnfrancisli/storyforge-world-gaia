Once a celebrated mechanic in the grand canal locks of [[location:sangguo]], Tie Luo was quietly forced out of the imperial engineering bureaus as the courts favored human-only guilds. He now operates a clandestine forge in the western hills, repairing heavy mining machinery in secret.
