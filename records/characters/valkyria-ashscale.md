High in the jagged volcanic ridges above [[location:dragonrest]], Valkyria keeps solitary watch over ancient wyrm-carved caves. With all male drakes vanished generations ago in the Silence of the Drakes, she preserves the lineage's oral codices alone, rarely descending unless the crags are disturbed.
