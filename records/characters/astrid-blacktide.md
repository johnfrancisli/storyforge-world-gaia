gm_notes: >-
  Astrid commands the longship Frostwing and is the most influential raid captain among
  [[org:hra-raid-crews|the Storm-Wing Crews]]. Three summers ago she drove the ship through
  [[location:border-basalt-skerries|the Basalt Skerries]] ahead of a black squall rather than abandon a
  rich prize; the Frostwing survived, but half her crew did not. Astrid still accepts responsibility in
  private, though in public she lets the loss be remembered as a heroic escape. After the wreck, a
  Valdrian widow in an isolated southern fishing village concealed and nursed Astrid until she could
  return north. On the Frostwing's most recent raid, Astrid recognized that woman among the villagers
  and ordered an abrupt withdrawal, falsely claiming that a militia column was approaching. Only
  [[character:halla-skiprope|Halla Skiprope]] saw the recognition on her face. Astrid has avoided that
  coast ever since. She remains a true believer in raiding as a source of Hrafnish wealth, but now argues
  for disciplined attacks on armed ships, fortified storehouses, and hostile ports rather than undefended
  settlements. If the crews learn that she spared a village for a personal debt, they may call it cowardice;
  if [[character:erik-stone-eye|Jarl Erik]] learns how deeply the lost voyage changed her, he may back a
  more reckless captain.

  A broad-shouldered, sea-hardened woman in dark ringmail beneath a seal-oil cloak, Astrid carries a round

biography: >-
  Astrid earned command young by bringing a dismasted longship home through winter seas after its captain
  was killed. She built the Frostwing's reputation through fast landings, strict division of spoils, and a
  standing order that no sailor be abandoned while recovery remained possible. That reputation made her a
  natural rallying point for [[org:hra-raid-crews|the Storm-Wing Crews]] at
  [[location:hra-skaldvik-quay|the Raven Quay]]. It also made her disastrous decision in
  [[location:border-basalt-skerries|the Basalt Skerries]] harder to confess: she gambled lives for plunder
  and survived because a stranger on the Valdrian coast chose mercy. Astrid returned north determined never
  to waste lives that way again. She still raids and still believes the fjords need the wealth, but she now
  measures success by how many crew return, bringing her into constant argument with younger glory-seekers
  and with [[character:ingrid-watchpeak|Ingrid Watchpeak]], whose defensive priorities threaten the raiding
  fleet's influence.
personality: >-

  people's lives carelessly. Her pride makes confession difficult, so remorse emerges as tighter discipline
  rather than open vulnerability.

The captain of the Frostwing and a leading voice among [[org:hra-raid-crews|the Storm-Wing Crews]], Astrid
Blacktide built her name on bringing sailors home. Now one concealed act of mercy on the southern coast
threatens to cost her the command she has spent half her life earning.
