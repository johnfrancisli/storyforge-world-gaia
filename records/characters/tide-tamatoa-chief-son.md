gm_notes: Tamatoa is the son of Chief Lani, though this is not widely known — his mother has kept their
  relationship out of public governance, and he has respected that by never invoking it. He is a fisherman
  on Lani Atoll, ordinary and unremarkable, and most people see him as just another face on the docks.
  His secret is that he knows why his mother is so worried about the rising seas, beyond the obvious.
  He found her private records — charts of water levels over twenty years, annotated with notes in his
  grandmother's handwriting. The notes go back three generations, and they show that the sea level has
  risen and fallen before — in a cycle. According to his grandmother's notes, the sea rises for about
  a generation, then falls, and the cycle has happened at least three times. But this time is different.
  The rate of rise is faster than any previous cycle, and his grandmother's last note, written in a shaking
  hand, says 'this is not the cycle. Something is pushing it.' Tamatoa has been trying to determine whether
  his grandmother was right or whether she was a frightened old woman, and he has been afraid to tell
  his mother what he found because it would confirm her worst fears.
