gm_notes: Vanya arrived at Lani Atoll eight months ago with her three children and nothing else. Her island,
  Vela, submerged in a single night — faster than anyone thought possible. She lost her husband, her parents,
  and her home in the same tide. She has been living in the refugee shelters and has become a quiet leader
  among the displaced, not by seeking authority but by being the one who organizes, mediates, and holds
  things together. Her secret is that she knows why Vela sank so fast. The night before it submerged,
  her husband — a pearl diver — came home from a dive and told her he had seen a crack in the sea floor,
  and light coming through it. He said the island was sitting on something hollow. He went back down the
  next morning to check, and the island sank that night. Vanya does not know if her husband's dive caused
  the collapse or if he was trying to close the crack. She has not told anyone because she is afraid the
  same thing is happening under other islands — and she is afraid someone will blame the refugees.
