A dwarven master smith in [[location:crownhaven|Crownhaven]]'s metalworking district. Compact, strong, and precise. He works with iron, steel, and dragonbone — one of the few smiths in Valdris who will touch the last. He is the person the knightly order comes to when their armor needs repair, and the person Elara comes to when she needs something made quietly.
