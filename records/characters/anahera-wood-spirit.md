  Not the silence of distance, which she knows. The silence of something covering the eyes of the carving.
  She has carved a replacement figurehead and is waiting for the canoe to return so she can compare them,
  but the canoe is overdue. She is afraid the crew is alive but the figurehead has been blinded by something
  that does not want to be seen.
