A round, jolly old man who runs a noodle shop in [[location:haru-shrine-town|Haru's Shrine Town]], famous for portions that are always generous and broth that makes people smile. He chats with every customer, remembers every name, and has been a fixture of the town for as long as anyone can remember. He is exactly what he appears to be — except that he isn't.
