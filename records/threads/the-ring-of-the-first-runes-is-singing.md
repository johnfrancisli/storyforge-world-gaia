The Ring of the First Runes, the oldest carved stones in Rúnarhólm, has begun to emit a low humming tone on windless nights that the carvers' bothies can hear through their walls. Volva Yrsa Fogborn, who came north to read the omen, has spent three nights at the ring and returned unable to speak above a whisper, writing that the runes are 'arguing with each other.' Rune carver Haldur Blackrune, summoned from Drekahöfn, has confirmed that two of the stones have altered their carvings overnight—marks no living hand cut. The Fog-Mother's Hut, the seat of the local volva, has begun attracting pilgrims from Eyrby who claim the humming cured an old injury.
