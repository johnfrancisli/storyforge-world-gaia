[[location:san-weidu-forge-quarter|The Forge Quarter]] of [[location:san-weidu|Weidu]] has begun producing blades of an unfamiliar dark metal that holds an edge longer than any steel the [[location:san-weidu-garrison-barracks|Stone Sentinel Barracks]] has tested. Iron mine foreman [[character:tian-baoshan|Tian Baoshan]], who secretly deals in qilin-iron, is the source—but he has tripled his price without explanation and refuses to name the new buyer who is outbidding the garrison. Army blacksmith [[character:zhao-tiegun|Zhao Tiegun]], himself a covert qilin-iron weapons dealer for the Chu state, has arrived in [[location:san-weidu|Weidu]] to find out who is cornering the supply. [[location:san-weidu-forge-quarter|The Forge Quarter]]'s master smiths are afraid to refuse either of them.
