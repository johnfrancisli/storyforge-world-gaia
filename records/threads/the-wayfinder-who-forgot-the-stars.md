Veteran wayfinder [[character:hoku|Hoku]], who has navigated the open sea for forty years, has begun forgetting the star-paths he carved into his own navigation sticks—paths he taught to every apprentice in the [[org:wayfinder-guilds|Wayfinder Guilds]]. He collapsed at the [[location:lani-atoll|Lani Atoll]]'s chief's float during a welcoming chant and could not complete the route-song for his own home island. Elder Wayfinder Pua, the song-keeper, reports that the chant Hoku was singing when he collapsed was not one anyone recognized, though its rhythm matched the tide-cycle perfectly. Pearl trader [[character:lio|Lio]], who had hired Hoku to guide a deep-water run, is now stranded on the atoll with a cargo that cannot wait.
