Three boatloads of salt cured at the Salt Market of [[location:ver-tidalreach|Tidal Reach]] have sickened inland trading partners, and the Saltwater Shrine's water-readers claim the brine itself has turned 'bitter' in the deep anchorage. Salt maker [[character:suri|Suri]], who leads the trade journeys, stands to lose the season's entire profit and possibly the consortium's inland contracts. Fisher [[character:toba|Toba]] insists the fish are still wholesome and that the bitterness is being added after the catch, not in the water. [[location:ver-tidalreach-tidemoothall|The Tide-Moot Hall]] has summoned both to answer, and the Anchorage fisher fleet is refusing to sail until the matter is settled.
