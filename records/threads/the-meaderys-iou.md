The Goldmalt Meadery in Eyrby, the finest mead-house in the southern fjords, owes a debt to a southern trader that it cannot pay in silver—only in a cask of mead aged since Jarl Sigrid's grandmother's time, a cask the meadery swore never to sell. Jarl Sigrid Hallvarsdottir, who counts the meadery as part of her holdings, has forbidden the sale and offered to cover the debt from her own strongbox, but the trader has refused, insisting on the cask or nothing. The Thing-Mound of Eyrby is set to hear the dispute at the next assembly, and the Winter-Eye Granary has quietly stopped advancing grain to the meadery until the matter is settled, threatening the winter brew.
