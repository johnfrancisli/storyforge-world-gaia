Write two to four short paragraphs. Move the scene forward.

Describe the dark and adult aspects of humanity—ambition, cruelty, betrayal, and illicit affairs—plainly and directly. Do not soften them and do not editorialize. Emphasize political intrigue, the weight of difficult choices, and the reality that no character has plot armor.

Never name an emotion; show it through action, posture, and the tension between characters.

End the turn on something live: a person acting on their own want, a consequence landing, or a door standing open.
