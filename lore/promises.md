# Promises

## Promises

- **Your choices close doors permanently.** If you offend someone, they remember. If you burn a bridge, it stays burned. The world does not reset between scenes.
- **The world moves without you.** Things happen while you are elsewhere. A war you ignored may have moved by the time you return. A friend you forgot to visit may have made other plans.
- **Betrayal is common and plot armor does not exist.** Characters will lie, cheat, and betray for their own agendas. Allies may become enemies when their interests shift, and no one is safe from the consequences of their actions.
- **No one is secretly a god.** People are people. Powerful people are powerful because of what they have done and what they control, not because of a hidden nature. Even the reincarnated are just people with unusual memories.
- **Magic has a cost.** Every form of magic in Gaia asks something of its user -- time, sacrifice, risk, relationship, or discipline. Nothing is free. Nothing is unlimited.
- **If an NPC says they will be somewhere, they will be there.** Characters are honest about their intentions. If they lie, the lie is intentional and discoverable, driven by their own ambition or deceit, not a convenience for the plot.
- **The world is worth exploring.** Every nation has its own beauty, its own food, its own way of being in the world. The story rewards curiosity.
- **Small moments matter.** A meal, a bath, a conversation, a festival -- these are not filler. They are the texture of a life, and the world treats them as real.

## Refusals

- No prophecy. No chosen one. No destiny.
- No grimdark purely for shock value. The world is dark, unforgiving, and explicitly tackles adult themes like cruelty, affairs, and illegitimacy, but these serve the narrative and character arcs rather than existing as nihilistic filler.
- No harem mechanics. Relationships are between people, not collectibles.
- No power fantasy escalation. Strength is earned, contextual, and never absolute. The strongest swordsman in Valdris can still drown.
- No fourth-wall breaks. The narrator never addresses the player.
- No sexual content involving minors. Non-negotiable.

## Scale

A week's travel is a meaningful journey within a nation. Crossing from one nation to another by conventional means is a month or more, depending on the route. [[lore:old-roads|The Old Roads]], when they work, collapse that to hours -- but finding a working stretch is rare, and using one always draws attention.

The largest city in Gaia is [[location:sangguo|Sangguo]]'s capital, [[location:jin-an|Jin'an]], with roughly two hundred thousand people. Valdris's capital, [[location:crownhaven|Crownhaven]], is near a hundred thousand. Most settlements the player will visit are towns of a few thousand or villages of a few hundred.

An army is thousands. A village militia is dozens. A band of adventurers is three to seven. The player is one person, and the world does not scale to them.
