# Races of Gaia

The people who share this world. Not all are found in every nation, but all
are known. Travel is common enough that most cities have at least a few
foreigners, and the [[lore:old-roads|Old Roads]], when they worked, moved
people as freely as goods.

## Distinct peoples and reproductive boundaries

Gaia's races are distinct peoples, not freely interchangeable branches of one
universally compatible species. Some mortal peoples may share a distant origin,
but long separation, adaptation, magic, and spirit influence have made their
modern bodies meaningfully different. A broadly humanlike shape does not by
itself establish reproductive compatibility.

Culture and geography preserve many of these distinctions. Some societies
strictly forbid marriage or childbearing outside their own people. Others lived
beyond one another's roads and seas and had no sustained contact until recent
generations. Even where two mortal peoples are anatomically compatible,
attraction or partnership does not guarantee that their families or communities
will accept children between them.

Other boundaries are physical. Differences in scale, anatomy, gestation, or
reproductive biology can make conception impossible or make intercourse or
pregnancy unsafe. Do not assume that any two races can have children merely
because both can take a humanoid form. Compatibility is specific to the two
peoples involved and must be established by canon rather than inferred.

Djinn, spirits, and yokai are supernatural beings rather than biological
breeding populations. They do not conceive, carry, sire, or bear children,
including while using a fully tangible humanlike form. Embodiment can still let
them touch the material world: they may fight or harm mortals, protect or aid
them, form friendships, experience love, tempt or seduce, and engage in
consensual sexual intimacy. None of those acts creates offspring.

## Rare mixed heritage

Mixed heritage exists only between mortal peoples whose compatibility is
explicitly established. It is uncommon because compatible groups may still be
separated by distance, law, custom, religion, family pressure, or lack of
contact. Never invent a mixed lineage to fill a gap in a genealogy.

When canon establishes such a child, inherited traits are individual rather
than a fixed fantasy taxonomy. Record the relevant ancestries and describe the
specific visible and physiological traits the person carries. Do not assume a
universal "half-elf" template, a predictable midpoint lifespan, or a generic
blend of animal features. For supernatural djinn, spirits, and yokai, use bonds,
blessings, curses, pacts, possession, or adopted and chosen family where canon
supports them; do not describe those relationships as biological descent.

## GM and prompt notes: ancestry, nation, and appearance

Treat **race and national heritage as two layers**, not as competing labels.
Race supplies inherited anatomy: pointed ears, compact stature, fur, scales,
webbing, horns, tails, wings, or other nonhuman traits. National and local
heritage supplies the broad human-coded range of complexion, facial features,
hair, height, dress, and grooming. A Sangguo elf should generally look like a
person of Sangguo with pronounced pointed ears, not like a pale Valdrian elf in
Sangguo clothing. The same rule applies to dwarves, halflings, and other peoples
whose skin and features are otherwise humanlike.

The real-world comparisons below are **out-of-world visual anchors for GMs,
artists, and image models**. They are not in-world ethnic names. They describe
population tendencies, never a checklist that every individual must satisfy.
Every nation contains short and tall people, light and dark complexions, many
body types, immigrants, and unusual inherited traits. Compatible mixed families
may exist where canon establishes them, but they are not universal. A
character's authored appearance always overrides a population baseline. Do not
change an established character merely to make them look more typical.

For a new character, apply appearance information in this order:

1. Use the character's explicitly authored physical traits.
2. Add the exact racial anatomy from [[lore:races-and-peoples-of-gaia]].
3. Use their nation, region, and family as a visual population baseline.
4. Add occupational wear, clothing, grooming, and individual variation.

### Tsukuyomi population baseline

[[location:tsukuyomi|Tsukuyomi]] is predominantly human, with visible yokai
communities, a small elven population, and individual cat-folk. Its human visual
anchor is **historical Japanese / East Asian**. Most Tsukuyomi humans have fair
beige through warm beige or golden-tan skin, dark brown eyes, and black or very
dark brown hair that is usually straight or softly wavy. Faces should read as
Japanese rather than as Europeans placed in Japanese clothing. Short-to-medium
adult height is common, but tall people are ordinary; builds range from slight
and compact to stocky, muscular, or full-figured. Fishing and farming
communities are often more sun-browned and weathered than court or indoor
populations.

Hair that has greyed, sun-faded, or gone white with age is common visual
variation. Naturally pale eyes or light hair should come from an explicit
individual trait, mixed heritage, magic, or nonhuman ancestry rather than being
the default. Yokai who pass as human normally share the same Japanese visual
baseline unless their record says their disguise copies another population.

### Sangguo population baseline

[[location:sangguo|Sangguo]] is predominantly human, with established elven
mountain communities and Mizuhito along its canals and rivers. Its human visual
anchor is **historical Chinese / East Asian**. Most Sangguo humans have light
beige, warm beige, golden, tan, or light-brown skin; dark brown to nearly black
eyes; and straight or softly wavy black or dark-brown hair. Faces should read as
Chinese rather than as Europeans in hanfu or armour. Adult height and build vary
widely, with compact, lean, sturdy, tall, and broad-shouldered people all
present.

Regional casting may add variety without making hard borders. Northern Jin
leans toward northern-Chinese visual ranges, including somewhat more light
beige complexions and tall or sturdy builds. Southern Chu more often shows warm
golden or tan complexions and compact or lean builds shaped by humid riverland
life. Western Wei and the highland frontier include a broad western-Chinese and
inland range, with wind-reddened or sun-browned skin and sturdy mountain builds
common. These are tendencies, not separate races; movement between the three
states is ancient and constant.

### Hrafnland population baseline

[[location:hrafnland|Hrafnland]] is predominantly human, with old elven and
dwarven communities. Frost giants are a giant-sized northern human population
of the deep mountains. The human visual anchor is **Norse / Scandinavian**. Fair,
pink-beige, and light skin are common, often freckled, wind-reddened,
salt-roughened, or tanned by outdoor work. Blond, ash-blond, red, and light-brown
hair occur at high frequency, while dark brown and black hair remain entirely
normal. Blue, grey, and green eyes are common, but brown eyes are also part of
the population.

Hrafnish people trend taller than Gaia's overall human average, and robust,
broad-shouldered, deep-chested, or long-limbed builds are common. Short, slight,
round, and wiry Hrafnish people are not exceptional. Beards are common adult
grooming for some communities and professions, not a male racial requirement;
do not add one when a character record does not specify it.

### Al-Khayzar population baseline

[[location:al-khayzar|Al-Khayzar]] is predominantly human, with a substantial
elven minority and rare djinn living openly or in mortal guise. Its human visual
anchor is **Arabian and Persian / West Asian**, with wider variation in coastal
ports and caravan cities. Complexions range from light olive and golden beige
through warm brown, bronze, and deep brown. Dark brown eyes and black or dark
brown hair are most common; hair may be straight, wavy, or curly, and thick
brows and strong or fine-featured West Asian faces should both appear.

The population spans short to tall and slight to broad builds. Desert travelers
often show sun-darkening, wind-weathering, and lean endurance builds; oasis and
city populations cover the full range. Mountain communities commonly show
weathered bronze or olive-brown complexions, while the coast reflects centuries
of sea trade. Do not reduce everyone to one generic "desert" skin tone, and do
not make every Al-Khayzari character pale simply because they are an elf.

### Tide Archipelago population baseline

[[location:tide-archipelago|The Tide Archipelago]] is predominantly human, with
long-established elven and cat-folk communities and rare sea-marked dragon-kin.
Its human visual anchors are **Polynesian and Southeast Asian**, varying from
island to island and often blending within wayfinder families. Complexions range
from light brown and golden brown through rich medium brown, bronze, and deep
brown. Dark brown eyes and black or dark-brown hair predominate; hair may be
straight, wavy, curly, or tightly curled, and sun and salt can lighten it toward
brown, copper, or blond. Faces should read as Polynesian or maritime Southeast
Asian according to island and family, not as Europeans given darker skin.

Height and body size vary by island. Some Polynesian-coded island populations
trend tall, broad, powerfully built, and deep-chested; some Southeast-Asian-coded
populations trend shorter, compact, or fine-boned. Lean swimmer, muscular
paddler, sturdy fisher, and soft or full-figured builds all belong. Tattoos can
record routes, genealogy, rank, vows, or life events, but tattoo patterns must
come from a character or community and should never be added as meaningless
decoration.

### Verdania population baseline

[[location:verdania|Verdania]] is one of Gaia's most visibly multi-racial
nations: humans, forest elves, Mizuhito, and several distinct beast-kin peoples
share its rivers and cities. Its human visual anchor is **Indigenous Amazonian
and tropical South American**, with additional variation in major river ports.
Complexions most often range from warm copper-brown and reddish brown through
rich brown and deep brown, though lighter and darker individuals both occur.
Black and dark-brown hair predominate and may be straight, wavy, curly, or
tightly curled; brown and dark-brown eyes are most common, with hazel or other
colours appearing individually. Faces should most often read as Indigenous
Amazonian or from the wider tropical South American range, not as Europeans
given a tan.

Short-to-medium height and compact, wiry, or athletic builds are common, but
tall, broad, muscular, round, and full-figured Verdanians are all established.
Canopy climbers, river crews, and long-distance runners may show occupation-
specific builds without those becoming ethnic rules. Body paint, featherwork,
tattoos, and ritual scarification carry specific local meanings. Use them when
the character or community establishes them, not as a generic "jungle"
costume.

### Valdris population baseline

[[location:valdris|Valdris]] is deliberately multi-racial: humans, elves,
dwarves, and halflings have large settled communities, and dragon-kin sometimes
appear in old bonded families. Its human visual anchor is **medieval European**
across a broad western, northern, central, and southern European range rather
than one uniformly English-looking population. Complexions range from pale and
freckled through beige, olive, weathered tan, and brown. Black, brown, auburn,
red, blond, and grey hair all occur; brown, blue, grey, green, hazel, and amber
eyes are all plausible.

Valdrian humans cover Gaia's full ordinary range of height and build. Northern
and highland families may overlap visually with Hrafnland, southern roads and
trade cities include more olive, tan, and brown complexions, and the royal and
merchant centers draw families from every nation. Do not use pale skin, blond
hair, or blue eyes as a universal shorthand for either Valdrian nationality or
high social status.

## Humans

Found everywhere. The most numerous race in Gaia and the most varied — a
human from [[location:hrafnland|Hrafnland]] and a human from
[[location:tsukuyomi|Tsukuyomi]] might share nothing but the shape of their
body. Humans are not the default; they are simply the most adaptable, and
they have spread the farthest. Humans live about seventy to ninety years.

Humans have no single canonical skin tone, face, hair colour, height, or body
size. Use the person's national and family baseline above. Across Gaia, most
adult humans fall roughly within ordinary real-world human height and body
ranges; an authored `short`, `medium`, or `tall` descriptor is more important
than a national average.

## Elves

Long-lived, slow to act, quick to remember. Elves live two to three centuries
and think in longer arcs than other races. They are most concentrated in
[[location:valdris|Valdris]], where they hold ancient forests and
some of the oldest noble houses, but elven communities exist in
[[location:verdania|Verdania]] (forest elves who are closer to the jungle
spirits) and in isolated enclaves across [[location:sangguo|Sangguo]]'s
mountains.

Elves are not inherently magical. Their longevity gives them time to master
things others learn only partially. An elven swordmaster has had a century to
practice. An elven scholar has read libraries that have since burned.

Elves have humanlike proportions and clearly pointed ears. They span the same
broad range of complexions, faces, hair, and body types as the national
populations among whom they live. Many are near human height; individuals may
be short, tall, slight, stocky, muscular, or full-figured. A graceful or
slender build is common in stories, not a biological requirement. Do not make
all elves pale, blond, luminous, youthful-looking, or delicate. Subtle skin
luminescence, unusual eyes, or other magical signs occur only when the
individual record establishes them.

## Dwarves

Stone-workers, metal-workers, and the people who build things that last.
Dwarves are most concentrated in Valdris's mountain holds and in
[[location:hrafnland|Hrafnland]]'s fjord cliffs, where their stonework
survives the freeze and thaw. Dwarven communities in
[[location:sangguo|Sangguo]] work the mountain mines of the west.

Dwarves live about a century and a half. They are shorter than most humans but
retain unmistakably adult proportions: broad through the torso, compact,
dense, and strong rather than childlike or comically squat. Individual height
still varies, and a tall dwarf may approach ordinary human height. A dwarf who
has never lifted a hammer is still harder to push over than a human who has.
Their complexion, facial structure, eyes, and hair follow family and national
heritage. Facial hair is individual and cultural, not universal.

## Halflings

Halflings are concentrated in Valdris, especially farming villages, river
towns, and market communities. Adults are distinctly short, commonly around
waist-to-chest height beside an average human, with compact adult proportions.
They may be thin, round, soft, wiry, or muscular; short stature does not mean a
child's face, oversized head, or immature body. Their skin tone, features, eyes,
and hair follow their family and national heritage.

## Cat-folk

Cat-folk are found most commonly in [[location:tsukuyomi|Tsukuyomi]], where
they are considered close to the spirit world, and in the
[[location:tide-archipelago|Tide Archipelago]], where fishing-cat
communities are among the best sailors in the Archipelago. They are also
present in [[location:verdania|Verdania]], where jungle cat-kin tribes are
among the most skilled hunters.

Cat-folk have cat ears, tails, slit-pupiled eyes, and a build that is
human-like but lighter and more agile. They are warm-blooded, social, and
expressive — ears and tail communicate mood as clearly as speech. They are
not domesticated cats. They are people who are also cats, and the distinction
matters.

Cat-folk are mortal people, not yokai. The name `nekomata` belongs to the true
cat spirits described below and should not be used as a biological synonym for
cat-folk. A cat-folk person may resemble a nekomata in silhouette, but the two
have different origins and reproductive natures.

Most are within the human range of height, often with compact, lean, or wiry
builds. Fur may cover only the ears and tail, form a fine pelt over parts of the
body, or cover the body more fully; the individual record decides. Coat colours
and markings vary as widely as natural cats. Cat-folk retain two arms and two
legs unless a record explicitly establishes another form. Do not confuse
cat-folk or nekomata with fox-kin or jaguar beast-kin.

## Beast-kin

A broad category for people who carry the traits of animals beyond cats.
Fox-kin in Tsukuyomi (sometimes called kitsune-kin), wolf-kin in
[[location:hrafnland|Hrafnland]], river-otter-kin in
[[location:verdania|Verdania]], hawk-kin in the
[[location:sangguo|Sangguo]] mountains. Beast-kin are not a single race —
they are many, and they do not share a culture. A fox-kin from Tsukuyomi and
a wolf-kin from [[location:hrafnland|Hrafnland]] have less in common than two
humans from those same places.

Beast-kin traits vary: some have full animal features (ears, tail, fur,
muzzle), others have subtle markers (unusual eyes, a faint pelt on the
forearms, a tail hidden under clothing). The variation is individual, not
hierarchical.

The animal lineage must be visually explicit; `beast-kin` alone is not enough
for an image prompt. Body scale usually remains humanoid, while build and
anatomy follow the lineage:

- **Otter-kin** are compact, sleek swimmers with dense brown fur, rounded ears,
  whiskers, and webbed clawed hands and feet.
- **Serpent-kin** are often lean or long-limbed, with visible facial and upper-
  body scales, vertical pupils, a forked tongue, and a scale crest instead of
  ordinary human hair. Scale colour and coverage vary.
- **Bird- and hawk-kin** are light-boned, with feathered crests and fine-scaled
  facial traits. Wings or long forearm feathers vary by lineage and individual;
  never add a second pair of arms merely to accommodate wings unless canon says
  so.
- **Jaguar-kin** tend toward agile, muscular builds with tawny fur or skin,
  dark rosettes, feline eyes, and elongated canines. Fur coverage varies.
- **Fox- and wolf-kin** carry the ears, tail, eyes, teeth, fur, and sometimes
  muzzle of the named animal. Coat colour and coverage are individual. Fox-kin
  are not automatically magical kitsune, and wolf-kin are not werewolves.

## Demon-folk (Mazoku)

The people of the demon lands — a harsh region south of Tsukuyomi across the
sea, sometimes called the Broken Continent. Demon-folk have skin tones
ranging from ash-grey to deep blue to mottled green, horns of various shapes,
and eyes that sometimes glow in low light. They are not evil. They are people
from a hard place, and the place has made them practical, direct, and
suspicious of politeness.

Demon-folk are rare outside their homeland. Those who travel are usually
adventurers, traders, or exiles. In most of Gaia they are met with curiosity
or wariness, occasionally hostility. In [[location:tsukuyomi|Tsukuyomi]] they
are technically barred from entry by an old edict that no one enforces.

Demon-folk are broadly human-sized and commonly show pronounced sexual
dimorphism. Adult women often have a succubus-like appearance: slender or
curvaceous feminine proportions, refined features, graceful limbs, and an
alluring or temptress-like presentation rather than the massive musculature
common among adult men. Adult men more often have tall, broad, heavy, or
strongly muscular builds and visibly imposing features. These are population
tendencies, not personality rules: an alluring-looking woman need not be
seductive, and individuals of any gender may depart from the usual build.

Their canonical skin range is ash-grey, deep blue, or mottled green rather than
ordinary human pink or beige. Horns may be straight, swept, curled, ridged,
paired, or asymmetrical; record their number, placement, colour, and damage on
the individual. Low-light eye glow varies. Succubus-like lineages may also have
narrow bat-like wings or a slender spade-tipped tail, but neither trait is
universal. Do not add wings, tails, hooves, tusks, or red skin unless the
individual or their established lineage supplies them.

## Dragon-kin (Ryujin)

Extremely rare. People who carry dragon blood, usually from an ancient pact
between a dragon and a human family. Dragon-kin have scale patches on their
skin, sometimes on the arms or neck, and eyes with vertical pupils. They are
slightly warm to the touch and have an instinctive sense for fire and
weather.

Dragon-kin are most common in
[[location:valdris|Valdris]] (where the dragon-bonded knightly
families sometimes produce them) and in [[location:sangguo|Sangguo]] (where
the dragon spirit-pacts occasionally leave a mark on the bloodline). In the
[[location:tide-archipelago|Tide Archipelago]], they are considered blessed
by the sea rather than the fire, and their scales are iridescent rather than
warm-colored.

Dragon-kin remain humanlike in overall height and build. Their ordinary skin,
face, and hair follow national and family heritage, overlaid by visible scale
patches and vertical pupils. Scale colour reflects the bloodline or pact:
warm-toned Valdrian lines, spirit-marked Sangguo lines, and iridescent sea tones
in the Tide Archipelago are all established. Horns, tails, wings, claws, and
full-body scales are not universal and must not be invented for an individual.

## River-folk (Mizuhito)

Amphibious people found in [[location:verdania|Verdania]] and the river
systems of [[location:sangguo|Sangguo]]. River-folk have smooth, slightly
scaled skin in blue-green tones, webbed fingers and toes, and can hold their
breath for extraordinary lengths of time. They are not merfolk — they walk on
land, live in houses, and participate in surface society. They simply also
belong to the water.

In [[location:verdania|Verdania]], river-folk are the majority population of
the river capital [[location:iara|Iara]]. In
[[location:sangguo|Sangguo]], they manage the canal systems that feed the
rice paddies.

Mizuhito are generally within the human height range, with compact, wiry,
broad-backed, or powerful swimmer's builds common. Their blue-green skin is an
ancestral trait and remains visible regardless of nation; it can range from
pale aqua through teal and deeper blue-green, with darker mottling on some
individuals. Webbed fingers and toes are invariant. Fine scales are subtle but
visible at useful portrait distance. Neck or rib gills, bioluminescent marks,
and other aquatic signs vary and should only be used when established.

## Yokai spirits of Tsukuyomi

Yokai are supernatural spirits with distinct anatomies, not one interchangeable
mortal race. They do not reproduce, even when tangible or concealed in a
convincingly human body. They may nevertheless form romantic or sexual
relationships with mortals or other spirits. Many can conceal their nonhuman
signs while moving through Tsukuyomi society. Use the explicitly stated current
form. In an ordinary human disguise, use Tsukuyomi's Japanese visual baseline;
in a natural or revealed form, retain the anatomy below.

- **Kitsune** are humanlike fox spirits. Their natural forms show fox ears,
  vulpine eyes or fangs, and one or more fox tails. Multiple tails reflect the
  individual, not an automatic age scale.
- **Tanuki** are humanlike raccoon-dog spirits with rounded ears, dark facial
  masks, broad or flat noses, and thick tails in natural form. They are not foxes.
- **Tengu** are avian humanoids, often tall or lean, with sharp avian features.
  Wings, temple feathers, pronounced noses, crests, and talon-like nails vary by
  individual and form.
- **Oni** are towering, massively built humanoids with one or more forehead
  horns and visible tusks. Iron-grey skin is established, while other skin and
  horn colours require individual or lineage support.
- **Nekomata** are cat yokai with feline ears, slit pupils, and one or more cat
  tails in natural form. They follow cat rather than fox or raccoon-dog anatomy.
- **Yuki-onna** appear as humanlike women with snow-pale, cool-toned skin and
  visible frost or supernatural cold in revealed form. They should not look warm
  or flushed unless successfully disguised.
- **Rokurokubi** appear human by day; their revealed form has a supernaturally
  elongated neck, often foreshadowed by a faint neck crease. They do not have
  serpent bodies or scales.

## Frost giants

Frost giants are humans adapted to [[location:hrafnland|Hrafnland]]'s deep
northern mountains, not a separate supernatural species. Adults stand about
twelve feet tall but otherwise have ordinary human anatomy and human variation
in faces, hair, eyes, and body shape. Their skin trends exceptionally fair and
pale because their homeland receives little sunlight; it is living human skin,
not cold grey, blue, stone-like, or corpse-like.

They must read at giant scale beside people, doors, trees, or architecture, not
as ordinary pale humans framed alone. Their remote habitat and immense size
keep them largely separate from other human populations. Cross-population
intimacy and pregnancy can also be physically unsafe because of the scale
difference, so frost-giant mixed families must never be assumed without explicit
canon.

## Djinn

Djinn are elemental fire spirits associated most closely with Al-Khayzar. They
do not reproduce, whether in their true forms or mortal guises, though a solid
manifestation can touch, fight, help, love, tempt, or engage in consensual sexual
intimacy. Their true forms are humanlike but visibly supernatural: flame or
ember signs, glowing cracks, smoke, heat shimmer, and unusual colour may reveal
the fire within. Height, apparent body size, flame colour, and solidity vary
widely. A djinn in a deliberate mortal disguise usually follows Al-Khayzar's
Arabian and Persian visual baseline and must not retain true-form signs unless
the disguise is failing or the character record asks for them.

## Spirits

`Spirit` covers many beings rather than a single anatomy. Spirits do not
reproduce, even when they can manifest a tangible body. Physical contact,
violence, aid, romance, temptation, and consensual sexual intimacy remain
possible according to the spirit's powers and choices, but cannot result in
biological children.

A visible spirit must retain some authored sign of incorporeal or elemental
nature: translucency, subtle luminosity, fading edges, floating hair or
clothing, an elemental texture, or another specific manifestation. Apparent
ancestry, age, height, and body shape come from the individual spirit. Do not
default every spirit to a blue translucent human, and do not render one as an
entirely ordinary living person unless it is explicitly disguised.
