# Races of Gaia

The people who share this world. Not all are found in every nation, but all are known. Travel is common enough that most cities have at least a few foreigners, and the Old Roads, when they worked, moved people as freely as goods.

## Humans

Found everywhere. The most numerous race in Gaia and the most varied -- a human from [[location:hrafnland|Hrafnland]] and a human from Tsukuyomi might share nothing but the shape of their body. Humans are not the default; they are simply the most adaptable, and they have spread the farthest.

## Elves

Long-lived, slow to act, quick to remember. Elves live two to three centuries and think in longer arcs than other races. They are most concentrated in Valdris, where they hold ancient forests and some of the oldest noble houses, but elven communities exist in [[location:verdania|Verdania]] (forest elves who are closer to the jungle spirits) and in isolated enclaves across [[location:sangguo|Sangguo]]'s mountains.

Elves are not inherently magical. Their longevity gives them time to master things others learn only partially. An elven swordmaster has had a century to practice. An elven scholar has read libraries that have since burned.

## Dwarves

Stone-workers, metal-workers, and the people who build things that last. Dwarves are most concentrated in Valdris's mountain holds and in [[location:hrafnland|Hrafnland]]'s fjord cliffs, where their stonework survives the freeze and thaw. Dwarven communities in [[location:sangguo|Sangguo]] work the mountain mines of the west.

Dwarves live about a century and a half. They are not short -- that is a misunderstanding from Valdris, where humans built wider. They are compact, dense, and strong. A dwarf who has never lifted a hammer is still harder to push over than a human who has.

## Cat-folk (Nekomata)

Cat-folk are found most commonly in Tsukuyomi, where they are considered close to the spirit world, and in the Tide Archipelago, where fishing-cat communities are among the best sailors in the Archipelago. They are also present in [[location:verdania|Verdania]], where jungle cat-kin tribes are among the most skilled hunters.

Cat-folk have cat ears, tails, slit-pupiled eyes, and a build that is human-like but lighter and more agile. They are warm-blooded, social, and expressive -- ears and tail communicate mood as clearly as speech. They are not domesticated cats. They are people who are also cats, and the distinction matters.

## Beast-kin

A broad category for people who carry the traits of animals beyond cats. Fox-kin in Tsukuyomi (sometimes called kitsune-kin), wolf-kin in [[location:hrafnland|Hrafnland]], river-otter-kin in [[location:verdania|Verdania]], hawk-kin in the [[location:sangguo|Sangguo]] mountains. Beast-kin are not a single race -- they are many, and they do not share a culture. A fox-kin from Tsukuyomi and a wolf-kin from [[location:hrafnland|Hrafnland]] have less in common than two humans from those same places.

Beast-kin traits vary: some have full animal features (ears, tail, fur, muzzle), others have subtle markers (unusual eyes, a faint pelt on the forearms, a tail hidden under clothing). The variation is individual, not hierarchical.

## Demon-folk (Mazoku)

The people of the demon lands -- a harsh region south of Tsukuyomi across the sea, sometimes called the Broken Continent. Demon-folk have skin tones ranging from ash-grey to deep blue to mottled green, horns of various shapes, and eyes that sometimes glow in low light. They are not evil. They are people from a hard place, and the place has made them practical, direct, and suspicious of politeness.

Demon-folk are rare outside their homeland. Those who travel are usually adventurers, traders, or exiles. In most of Gaia they are met with curiosity or wariness, occasionally hostility. In Tsukuyomi they are technically barred from entry by an old edict that no one enforces.

## Dragon-kin (Ryujin)

Extremely rare. People who carry dragon blood, usually from an ancient pact between a dragon and a human family. Dragon-kin have scale patches on their skin, sometimes on the arms or neck, and eyes with vertical pupils. They are slightly warm to the touch and have an instinctive sense for fire and weather.

Dragon-kin are most common in Valdris (where the dragon-bonded knightly families sometimes produce them) and in [[location:sangguo|Sangguo]] (where the dragon spirit-pacts occasionally leave a mark on the bloodline). In the Tide Archipelago, they are considered blessed by the sea rather than the fire, and their scales are iridescent rather than warm-colored.

## River-folk (Mizuhito)

Amphibious people found in [[location:verdania|Verdania]] and the river systems of [[location:sangguo|Sangguo]]. River-folk have smooth, slightly scaled skin in blue-green tones, webbed fingers and toes, and can hold their breath for extraordinary lengths of time. They are not merfolk -- they walk on land, live in houses, and participate in surface society. They simply also belong to the water.

In [[location:verdania|Verdania]], river-folk are the majority population of the river capital [[location:iara|Iara]]. In [[location:sangguo|Sangguo]], they manage the canal systems that feed the rice paddies.
